A torsion-free counterexample to the Kadison–Kaplansky conjecture

Kadison and Kaplansky conjectured that the reduced group C*-algebra of a torsion-free group contains no projections other than 0 and 1. OpenAI constructed in 2026 a finitely generated torsion-free group with a projection whose canonical trace lies strictly between 0 and 1/2. The group is defined by the closed walks of a voltage cover of an infinite layered expander, the tower, with voltages in a torsion-free nilpotent group; a weighted adjacency matrix over its group ring has a separated spectral band of small trace, and compression after a free product gives the scalar projection. The tower is built from streams and gated permutations and pruned so that long paths are recovered from their words; the voltages realize a model operator assembled from Schwartz idempotents of twisted group algebras, with one random draw per edge. The course is an exposition of OpenAI's proof (2026).

18 lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.
