Closing a domain after spectral localization

OA-FLOW.GRAPH.CONTRACT — Functional calculus used here

Application to a dual Hilbert algebra. Suppose a proposed left Hilbert algebra has dense underlying vector domain DDD, an antilinear operator S0S_0S0​ on DDD, and operators J,ΔJ,\DeltaJ,Δ with JJJ antiunitary, Δ\DeltaΔ positive nonsingular self-adjoint, and

The isometry is real-linear on H⊕HH\oplus HH⊕H if JJJ is antilinear; this suffices to preserve closure and convergence of graphs. It also proves closability directly. This closes the analytic bridge once all the displayed hypotheses have been proved for the dual Hilbert algebra. It does not establish its density, invariance or the displayed formula; those remain concrete obligations in the general dual-weight construction.
