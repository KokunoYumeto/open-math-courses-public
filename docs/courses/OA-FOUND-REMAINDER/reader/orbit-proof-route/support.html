ORBIT-BRIDGE-SPECTRAL-REDUCTION — The spectral projections follow the unitary group

Let Ut=eitPU_t=e^{itP}Ut​=eitP be a strongly continuous unitary group on an arbitrary Hilbert space. If a closed subspace K reduces every U_t, then K reduces every spectral projection of P.

The exact inputs are Stone's theorem SG04, its two vector Laplace resolvents SG13–14, and the bounded Borel calculus/real change of variable of SK04/SK06/SK08. No group Fourier transform is assumed.

Faithfulness of multiplication follows by testing finite-measure subsets of a nonzero multiplier's support. ORBIT-BRIDGE-AMPLIFICATION-SLICE — The tensor facts in BF77 E8–E9

For arbitrary Hilbert L and a concrete von Neumann algebra A on K, (1L⊗A)′=B(L)⊗ˉA′(1_L\otimes A)'=B(L)\bar\otimes A'(1L​⊗A)′=B(L)⊗ˉ​A′. For a unit e in L, the vector slice of B(L)⊗ˉAB(L)\bar\otimes AB(L)⊗ˉ​A belongs to A and is normal.

Proof. Choose an arbitrary orthonormal basis of L. Matrix entries of an operator commuting with 1⊗A1\otimes A1⊗A lie in A'. Compress to finite basis subsets: each compression is a finite matrix over A' and hence belongs to B(L)⊗ˉA′B(L)\bar\otimes A'B(L)⊗ˉ​A′. These compressions converge strongly to the operator, over the directed set of finite subsets. The algebra is strongly closed; this proves the nontrivial inclusion. Elementary tensors commute with 1⊗A1\otimes A1⊗A, and taking their strong closure gives the reverse inclusion. No basis enumeration is used.

For the slice let W:K→L⊗KW:K\to L\otimes KW:K→L⊗K be Wξ=e⊗ξW\xi=e\otimes\xiWξ=e⊗ξ. The slice is W∗XWW^*XWW∗XW. It belongs to A: for y∈A′y\in A'y∈A′, Wy=(1⊗y)WWy=(1\otimes y)WWy=(1⊗y)W, and every X in the tensor algebra commutes with 1⊗y1\otimes y1⊗y. Hence W∗XWW^*XWW∗XW commutes with A', so is in A. Its pairings are vector pairings ⟨X(e⊗ξ),e⊗η⟩\langle X(e\otimes\xi),e\otimes\eta\rangle⟨X(e⊗ξ),e⊗η⟩, and the same formula for square-summable vector pairs proves ultraweak continuity and normality on arbitrary L and K. Finally the slice of 1⊗x1\otimes x1⊗x is x, which is precisely the removal of amplification in E9. ∎
