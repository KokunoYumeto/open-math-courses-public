Changing reference for a weight cocycle

The ordered chain rule

Theorem. Let α,β,γ\alpha,\beta,\gammaα,β,γ be faithful normal semifinite weights on a von Neumann algebra MMM. For every real ttt, [Dγ:Dα]t=[Dγ:Dβ]t[Dβ:Dα]t.(CH.1) [D\gamma:D\alpha]_t =[D\gamma:D\beta]_t[D\beta:D\alpha]_t. \tag{CH.1} [Dγ:Dα]t​=[Dγ:Dβ]t​[Dβ:Dα]t​.(CH.1) The factors need not commute.

Proof. Represent MMM faithfully and nondegenerately on a Hilbert space HHH, and put N=M′N=M'N=M′. Choose a faithful normal semifinite weight ω\omegaω on NNN. All three spatial derivatives can then be taken on the same HHH: A=dαdω,B=dβdω,C=dγdω. A=\frac{d\alpha}{d\omega},\qquad B=\frac{d\beta}{d\omega},\qquad C=\frac{d\gamma}{d\omega}. A=dωdα​,B=dωdβ​,C=dωdγ​. They are positive, injective, self-adjoint operators. Their imaginary powers are bounded unitaries on all of HHH, even if the derivatives and their inverses are unbounded. The spatial formula for the balanced-weight cocycle gives [Dγ:Dβ]t[Dβ:Dα]t=(CitB−it)(BitA−it)=CitA−it=[Dγ:Dα]t. \begin{aligned} [D\gamma:D\beta]_t[D\beta:D\alpha]_t &=(C^{it}B^{-it})(B^{it}A^{-it})\\ &=C^{it}A^{-it}\\ &=[D\gamma:D\alpha]_t. \end{aligned} [Dγ:Dβ]t​[Dβ:Dα]t​​=(CitB−it)(BitA−it)=CitA−it=[Dγ:Dα]t​.​ Only the adjacent factors involving BBB cancel. No unbounded operators are moved across one another, and no common domain for their products is needed. Each cocycle in this computation lies in MMM and is independent of the commutant weight and of the faithful representation. Faithfulness of the representation proves the identity in MMM. The zero algebra has the same identity in its zero-dimensional representation. □\square□

Reversal and paths of reference changes

Taking all three weights equal in the theorem gives the identity cocycle. Taking γ=α\gamma=\alphaγ=α then gives the reverse comparison: [Dα:Dα]t=1,[Dα:Dβ]t=[Dβ:Dα]t∗.(CH.2) [D\alpha:D\alpha]_t=1,\qquad [D\alpha:D\beta]_t=[D\beta:D\alpha]_t^*. \tag{CH.2} [Dα:Dα]t​=1,[Dα:Dβ]t​=[Dβ:Dα]t∗​.(CH.2) Indeed, the spatial formula yields AitA−it=1A^{it}A^{-it}=1AitA−it=1, and each cocycle is unitary.
