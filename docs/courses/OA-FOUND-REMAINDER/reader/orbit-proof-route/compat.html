Exact domain instantiation for the preserved commutant proof

The graph, polar and spectral results in Closing an involution and recovering its modular data, Spectral calculus with its domains retained and Recovering operators from energy forms give the domain identities used below.

Let C⊆H\mathcal C\subseteq HC⊆H be an arbitrary left Hilbert algebra with the four HA01 axioms. It need not be unital, full or separable. Its algebraic involution sss is closable by its hypothesis. TC03 and TC05 give S=s‾S=\overline sS=s, F=S∗F=S^*F=S∗, closed dense domains, S−1=SS^{-1}=SS−1=S on its actual range, and the graph core C\mathcal CC. TC07–10 apply to this SSS without a vector-model premise. Thus every equality in MF.3–4 is available from these operator results with its exact domain:

Δ=FS,S=JΔ1/2,F=JΔ−1/2,SF=Δ−1,J2=I,JΔzJ=Δ−zˉ. \Delta=FS,\quad S=J\Delta^{1/2},\quad F=J\Delta^{-1/2},\quad SF=\Delta^{-1},\quad J^2=I,\quad J\Delta^zJ=\Delta^{-\bar z}. Δ=FS,S=JΔ1/2,F=JΔ−1/2,SF=Δ−1,J2=I,JΔzJ=Δ−zˉ.

Ordinary linear graph identities used by HA07 and RD02

TC03 proves the linear graph identity before its conjugate-linear specialization:

G(T)⊥={(−T∗y,y):y∈D(T∗)} G(T)^\perp=\{(-T^*y,y):y\in D(T^*)\} G(T)⊥={(−T∗y,y):y∈D(T∗)}

for any densely defined linear T:H→KT:H\to KT:H→K. It proves that closability is equivalent to density of D(T∗)D(T^*)D(T∗). The ordinary adjoint is closed by its defining pairings: if yn→yy_n\to yyn​→y and T∗yn→zT^*y_n\to zT∗yn​→z, then ⟨Tx,y⟩=⟨x,z⟩\langle Tx,y\rangle=\langle x,z\rangle⟨Tx,y⟩=⟨x,z⟩ for every x∈D(T)x\in D(T)x∈D(T). The pairings defining T∗T^*T∗ extend to graph limits, giving (T‾)∗=T∗(\overline T)^*=T^*(T)∗=T∗. Finally, if TTT is closable, a pair (x,z)(x,z)(x,z) is orthogonal to every (−T∗y,y)(-T^*y,y)(−T∗y,y) precisely when

⟨x,T∗y⟩=⟨z,y⟩(y∈D(T∗)). \langle x,T^*y\rangle=\langle z,y\rangle \quad(y\in D(T^*)). ⟨x,T∗y⟩=⟨z,y⟩(y∈D(T∗)).

These are consequences of the graph identities just proved. The sequence arguments use the graph's metric, with no assumption on the dimension of HHH or KKK.

Boundary of the domain argument

The graph, spectral and closed-form results above do not assert the algebraic commutant identity. That higher result uses Building the two multiplication actions of a Hilbert algebra, Recovering the commutant from right-bounded vectors, Weights and the Hilbert spaces of multiplication, the analytic arguments in Analytic kernels for unbounded modular operators, and The modular group and its analytic algebra. The original-weight, relative-operator and crossed-product coefficient-domain arguments require their additional stated hypotheses and proofs.
