Normal comparison and uniqueness

Let rho and sigma be faithful normal unital representations of M, on arbitrary H_rho and H_sigma. Their concrete coefficient algebras are normally isomorphic through γ=σρ−1\gamma=\sigma\rho^{-1}γ=σρ−1. The spatial supplement, Corollary 8.4, supplies a normal isomorphism with normal inverse

Θ=γ⊗ˉid⁡:ρ(M)⊗ˉB(ℓ2(G))⟶σ(M)⊗ˉB(ℓ2(G)).(R15) \Theta=\gamma\bar\otimes\operatorname{id}: \rho(M)\bar\otimes B(\ell^2(G)) \longrightarrow\sigma(M)\bar\otimes B(\ell^2(G)). \tag{R15} Θ=γ⊗ˉ​id:ρ(M)⊗ˉ​B(ℓ2(G))⟶σ(M)⊗ˉ​B(ℓ2(G)).(R15)

For an arbitrary T, the bounded finite compressions tend ultraweakly to T, and normality and normal coordinate compression extend that equality to every entry. Relabeling the coefficient system

Let eta:M→P be a normal isomorphism intertwining alpha with an action beta of the same discrete group. Given faithful normal unital rho of M and sigma of P, replace gamma by σηρ−1\sigma\eta\rho^{-1}σηρ−1 in the preceding tensor construction. Its diagonal entries are

It follows that there is a unique normal isomorphism

Cη:Nρα⟶Nσβ,Cη(πρα(a))=πσβ(η(a)),Cη(Lgρ)=Lgσ.(R17) C_\eta:N_\rho^\alpha\longrightarrow N_\sigma^\beta, \quad C_\eta(\pi_\rho^\alpha(a))=\pi_\sigma^\beta(\eta(a)), \quad C_\eta(L_g^\rho)=L_g^\sigma. \tag{R17} Cη​:Nρα​⟶Nσβ​,Cη​(πρα​(a))=πσβ​(η(a)),Cη​(Lgρ​)=Lgσ​.(R17)

Uniqueness proves composition compatibility. A degenerate coefficient representation must first be compressed to rho(1)H_rho: its uncompressed regular algebra can have an extra group-algebra summand, so the displayed unital comparison is not asserted there. The zero algebra has its unique zero interpretation.

The result here is normal model independence. It does not assert the arbitrary-covariant commutant formula E9, a standard-form commutant, or a normal extension of every covariant representation. Those statements retain their separate proof obligations.
