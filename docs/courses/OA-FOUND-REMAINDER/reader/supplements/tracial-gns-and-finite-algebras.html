Prerequisites and notation

Inner products are linear in the first variable. Normal vector functionals, bounded separate continuity of multiplication, and ultraweak density in the bicommutant are proved in The double commutant theorem. Fact 2.5(1) means the finite-trace extension and trace identity proved in Proposition 2.2 of Traces, part A.
