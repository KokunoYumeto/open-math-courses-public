Kirchberg's embedding problem

Kirchberg's embedding problem: does every separable C*-algebra embed into the norm ultrapower of the Cuntz algebra O₂? The answer is no: the full C*-algebra of an explicit group has no unital embedding into the norm ultrapower of any nuclear C*-algebra. The proof: a spectral test for representations of a group with property (T), a finiteness statement in nuclear algebras, and an endomorphism of finite index that forces chains of representations. Proved in full from two named hypotheses: property (T) of the group ℤ³ ⋊ SL₃(ℤ), and the nuclearity of O₂. The course is an exposition of OpenAI's proof (2026).

Three lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  1. Norm ultrapowers and a group with property (T)
  2. A spectral test and nuclear finiteness
  3. Predecessors and the contradiction
