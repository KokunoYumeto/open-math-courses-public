Derivators

Grothendieck's proposal in Pursuing Stacks to keep all derived categories of diagram categories at once, with restriction functors that have adjoints on both sides. Why cones in a derived category cannot be chosen functorially; localization, prederivators, Kan's formulas and the derivator axioms; homotopy exact squares; the derivator of a ring, its strongness and its stability; recognition of cocartesian squares and shifted derivators; additivity of stable derivators, with the flip of a suspension equal to -1; the canonical triangulated structure, exact functors, the Dold-Puppe sequences, and the comparison with the classical triangles of the derived category of a ring, sign included.

Nine lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  1. Diagrams up to weak equivalence
  2. Kan extensions and the derivator axioms
  3. Homotopy exact squares
  4. The derivator of a ring
  5. Pointed and stable derivators
  6. Recognizing cocartesian squares
  7. Stable derivators are additive
  8. The canonical triangulated structure
  9. The triangles of the derivator of a ring
