Spectral triples and the local index formula

Begin with the spectral budget of a compact operator, then learn how regular commutators support complex powers and residue traces. Study index pairings alongside the weighted geometry of transverse directions.

  1. Singular values and the Dixmier trace
  2. Spectral triples and dimension spectrum
  3. The local index formula
  4. Symbol calculus, transverse geometry and geometric index formulas

The first two lessons begin with the analytic foundations they use. The final geometry lesson develops symbol estimates, coordinate transport, parametrices and closed domains before proving the classical trace formula and the geometric Dirac specialization.
