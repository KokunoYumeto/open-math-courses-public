The two unmodified DejaVu Sans fonts retain their complete DejaVu/Bitstream/Arev terms in FONT-NOTICE.txt. The generator selects the bundled files and embeds the complete notice in the PNG and SVG. These component terms also apply to their outlined SVG glyphs.

Python, Matplotlib, NumPy and Pillow are required to run the generator. Their runtimes are not redistributed here. Their complete licence notices are included as PYTHON-LICENSE.txt, MATPLOTLIB-LICENSE.txt, NUMPY-LICENSE.txt and PILLOW-LICENSE.txt.
