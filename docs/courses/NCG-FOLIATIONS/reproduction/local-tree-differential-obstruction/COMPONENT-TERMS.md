The unmodified DejaVu Sans fonts in fonts/ retain the complete DejaVu/Bitstream terms in FONT-NOTICE.txt. The notice is also included in the SVG description and PNG metadata. Those terms apply to the font outlines.

Python, Matplotlib, NumPy and Pillow are optional reproduction dependencies. Their runtimes are not distributed in this directory. Their complete primary licence texts accompany the generator in PYTHON-LICENSE.txt, MATPLOTLIB-LICENSE.txt, NUMPY-LICENSE.txt and PILLOW-LICENSE.txt. The package versions are listed in requirements.txt.
