# Component terms

The independently written proofs, solved exercises, captions, original mathematical diagrams and generator are dedicated under CC0 1.0. The complete dedication is in LICENSE-CC0.txt. Mathematical sources and earlier programme proofs are credited in the lesson and keep their own terms.

The single unmodified DejaVuSans.ttf retains the complete Bitstream Vera, DejaVu and applicable Arev terms in FONT-NOTICE.txt. The font and full notice are embedded in both SVGs. The raster diagrams use that same font; its terms remain distinct from the original diagram dedication.

Python and Pillow runtimes are not bundled. Their complete corresponding notices, including Pillow component notices, are in software-notices/Python-LICENSE.txt and software-notices/Pillow-LICENSE.txt. requirements.txt records the Pillow version. The generator uses Pillow BASIC font layout and includes no source-page pixels.
