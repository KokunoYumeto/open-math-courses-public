# Proper groupoid coefficients: full and reduced norms

Section 11U of *K-theory of the leaf space* constructs the source-column Hilbert module, completed double-arrow interior module and exact even cutoff isometry. Its full inner-product and intertwining calculations prove that the actual full-to-reduced quotient is a graded isomorphism for every separable graded proper central coefficient of a second-countable locally compact Hausdorff etale groupoid. The geometric cutoff is proved in Theorem 11S.3. Exercises 157–159 give complete solutions. Original-unit Dirac factors and the graph operator remain separate requirements.
