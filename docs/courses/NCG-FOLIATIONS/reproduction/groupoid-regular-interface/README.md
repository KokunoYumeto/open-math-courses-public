# Regular fields and the graph compactness test

Section 11M of *K-theory of the leaf space* proves the correctly typed regular-field reduced norm on eligible metric holonomy transversals, automatic Hausdorffness, the distinction between a diagonal corner and a noncentral balanced quotient, and the failure of localized scalar compactness for an unconfined normal operator on an actual smooth suspension graph. The complete proofs and three solved exercises are in the lesson.

Figure 11M.1 shows the finite corner and zero quotient, the exact coefficient transport triangle, the whole source-line multiplicity and a finite sample of the proved infinite orthonormal sequence. The normal circle and finite Clifford block are suppressed only in the geometric panel. Exact coordinates, phases, support bounds and image distances are retained in FIGURE-DATA.json. The finite diagram does not replace the infinite proof.
