# Component terms

The mathematical diagram, caption and generator are dedicated under CC0 1.0. LICENSE-CC0.txt contains the complete legal text. Linked prerequisite readings retain their own source credits and terms.

The unmodified DejaVu Sans font retains its complete Bitstream Vera, DejaVu and Arev terms in FONT-NOTICE.txt. The SVG embeds this font and notice. Its terms remain separate from the CC0 dedication.

Python 3.13 and Pillow 12.2.0 are needed to run the generator; their runtimes are not distributed here. The complete Pillow/PIL and bundled component terms are in software-notices/Pillow-LICENSE.txt.
