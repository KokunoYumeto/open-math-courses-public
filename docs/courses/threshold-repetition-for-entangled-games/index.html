Threshold repetition for entangled games

For every finite two-player one-round game with entangled value v < 1, the probability of winning at least a fraction v + delta of k parallel repetitions decays exponentially in k, uniformly over all finite-dimensional entangled strategies and correlated question distributions. The proof: sampling tested coordinates turns a conditioning estimate into a threshold bound; resolvent purifications put conditioned states in common spaces with exact answer probabilities; an entropy telescope controls how they depend on the other player's question; one reference vector per support component yields legal one-game strategies and a delta^3 rate. A universal delta^5 rate rests on Song's conditioning estimate, a named hypothesis. The course is an exposition of OpenAI's proof (2026).

Five lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.
