Finite symmetric tensor categories and the Verlinde tower

Every finite symmetric tensor category in characteristic p > 0 admits a fibre functor to a higher Verlinde category, the finite case of the Benson-Etingof-Ostrik conjecture, after OpenAI's 2026 manuscript. Tilting modules for SL_2; an equivariant vector bundle on the plane and the restricted tilting theorem; higher Frobenius functors; norm maps and symmetrizers; rank varieties; descent through Frobenius-Perron dimensions; incompressible categories and reconstruction.

Nine lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  1. Finite symmetric tensor categories and fibre functors
  2. Rational representations of SL₂ and tilting modules
  3. Lifting representations of elementary abelian groups to the plane
  4. The restricted tilting theorem
  5. Higher Frobenius functors
  6. Norm maps and a first nonprojective tensor power
  7. Rank varieties and an exact higher Frobenius functor
  8. Descent to a Verlinde category
  9. Incompressible categories, uniqueness and reconstruction
