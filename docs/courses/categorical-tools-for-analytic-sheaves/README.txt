Categorical and derived tools for analytic sheaves

An English course with 236 lessons and 944 fully worked exercises. Topics include universal properties, diagrams and exactness, representability, Ind and Pro constructions, tensor and duality, modules and twisted sheaves, complexes, triangulated categories, derived Hom, compatible derived functors, injectivity, homological dimension and short exact extensions with Baer sums and higher Yoneda composition and thick-subcategory derived comparison, functorial cones and derived connecting maps, two-cohomology Ext classes and polynomial models, finite cohomology splittings and derived kernels, right derived tensor over principal ideal domains, cosyzygy stabilization and the bounded injective quotient, hard-Lefschetz cohomology splitting, the even-Euler null system in the bounded derived category, formal Pro injectives with their minimal hypotheses, Weyl polynomial modules and their Koszul Tor and Ext calculations, polynomial finite resolutions and derived duality, hereditary cohomology and integral tensor splittings, nested Hom classes and bounded derived embeddings. Further topics will be added.
