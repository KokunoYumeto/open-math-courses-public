Categorical and derived tools for analytic sheaves

An English course with 237 lessons and 948 fully worked exercises. Universal properties and category theory; Ind and Pro constructions; monoidal categories and duality; modules, stacks and coherent descent; complexes, triangulated categories and derived functors; sheaf cohomology, local-to-global comparisons and gerbes.

Open index.html to read the complete course. Each lesson states its mathematical hypotheses and prerequisites and includes four graded exercises with full solutions.
