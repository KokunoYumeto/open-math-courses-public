Lie's theory of transformation groups

Analytic local transformation groups, parameters, flows, complete systems, the three fundamental theorems, structure and homogeneous actions, jets and contact transformations, classification, and the Riemann–Helmholtz problem. Twelve lesson drafts with independently written proofs and solved exercises are available. Assigned historical/source work and the full Lie–Tresse and classification derivations remain in progress.

  1. Transformation groups and their parameters
  2. The fundamental differential equations
  3. One-parameter groups
  4. Complete systems and invariants
  5. The second fundamental theorem and the group of parameters
  6. The third fundamental theorem
  7. The adjoint group, composition and isomorphism
  8. Transitivity and primitivity
  9. Prolongation, differential invariants and the projective group
  10. Contact transformations and first-order equations
  11. Lie algebras of vector fields on the line and in the plane
  12. The Riemann-Helmholtz space problem
