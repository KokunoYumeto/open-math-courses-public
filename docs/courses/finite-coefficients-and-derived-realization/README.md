# Finite coefficients and derived realization

A proof of finite-heart realization for constructible sheaves with appropriate ind-finite injectives, including a direct Artin–Rees and Baer construction for finitely generated commutative monodromy algebras. The reading also proves derived comparisons for ideal-power torsion and zero-dimensional support, and the finite-stalk projective-line torus theorem over every field. Four exercises have complete solutions.
