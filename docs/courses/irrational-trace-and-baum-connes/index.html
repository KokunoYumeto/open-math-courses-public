A projection of irrational trace and the Baum–Connes conjecture

The reduced group C*-algebra of a countable group carries a canonical trace, and the Baum-Connes conjecture predicts its K-theory from proper actions. By a theorem of Lück every predicted class has a rational trace. OpenAI constructed in 2026 a finitely generated group with a projection in its reduced group C*-algebra whose trace is the irrational number sum p^(-k_i), so the reduced Baum-Connes conjecture without coefficients fails. The group is a semidirect product of a graphical small cancellation group, whose Cayley graph contains labelled expanders isometrically, with a module over F_p defined by linear codes on the copies of the expanders. A Fourier transform turns a Laplacian-type element into random subgraph Laplacians with a uniform spectral gap, and its kernel projection counts the copies whose random pattern is the prototype. The course is an exposition of OpenAI's proof (2026).

Seven lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.
