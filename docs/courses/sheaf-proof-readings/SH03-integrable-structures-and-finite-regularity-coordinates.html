Integrable structures and finite-regularity coordinates

This reading constructs compatible complex coordinates for an integrable almost-complex structure of finite regularity. Two polynomial coordinate changes, a weighted solution of the Cauchy–Riemann equations and finite elliptic regularity give the coordinate theorem. The final section retains the holomorphic parameters in a Picard-zero deformation of the Fermat quartic. The construction uses no deformation-existence theorem and assumes no smooth bootstrap of the original almost-complex structure.

1. Statement and regularity budget

Let MM be a smooth real manifold of dimension d=2nd=2n, and let J∈C5,1/2(End⁡TM)J\in C^{5,1/2}(\operatorname{End}TM) satisfy J2=−1J^2=-1. Suppose that sections of TJ0,1MT^{0,1}_J M are closed under the Lie bracket. Each point has a neighbourhood with a C2,1/4C^{2,1/4} diffeomorphism to an open set of ℂn\mathbb C^n whose differential intertwines JJ and multiplication by ii. The changes between these coordinates are holomorphic.

When n=0n=0 the assertion is immediate on the discrete zero-dimensional manifold. Henceforth assume n≥1n\geq1.

Here and below a smaller Hölder exponent is harmless. The conclusion asserted is deliberately weaker than optimal finite-regularity Newlander–Nirenberg estimates. It is enough for the real dimension 44 used by the family construction.

More generally, if h=(h1,…,hb):M→ℂbh=(h_1,\ldots,h_b):M\to\mathbb C^b is C2,1/4C^{2,1/4}, annihilated by TJ0,1T^{0,1}_J, and has real rank 2b2b, then the coordinates can be chosen as (w1,…,wn−b,h1,…,hb)(w_1,\ldots,w_{n-b},h_1,\ldots,h_b).

The proof uses only two spatial Taylor cancellations. The local Kähler metric constructed below has class C4,1/2C^{4,1/2}, its connection has class at least C3,1/2C^{3,1/2}, and its curvature has class at least C2,1/2C^{2,1/2}. The final scalar elliptic argument uses coefficients of class C2,1/2C^{2,1/2}, an L2L^2 distributional solution and a finite sequence of LpL^p improvements. There is no Sobolev embedding from a fixed small HsH^s into C1C^1 in real dimension 44.

7. Vanishing of the first jet and coordinate compatibility
