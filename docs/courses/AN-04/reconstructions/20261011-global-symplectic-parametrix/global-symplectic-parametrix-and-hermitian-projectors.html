A global symplectic parametrix with Hermitian projectors

The local complex model has an inverse modulo two projections. To assemble it over a manifold, we must place the partition of unity on different sides in the two characteristic regions. To obtain a canonical answer, we must then make the projections self-adjoint. This lesson proves both operations, including the regularity of the inverse used in that normalization.

The mathematical antecedent is Lars Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators IV, 2009 edition, Section 26.3, final paragraph on printed page 91. The model-parametrix lesson, SMP:S2, supplies the full local operators. The endpoint companion, CX:E0–E4, supplies the proved finite-seminorm packet estimate and dyadic summation. The global graph companion, GE:G0, supplies locally finite conic partitions with proper kernel supports. We prove the additional commutator and inverse assertions required here.
