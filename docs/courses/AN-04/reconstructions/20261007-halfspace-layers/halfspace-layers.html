Half-space operators and complete boundary sources

An inverse on a doubled collar must still act on a zero-extended right-hand side and on the source concentrated at the boundary. Those are different inputs. This companion proves the two maps, calculates the entire boundary source of the original variable-coefficient operator, and retains the precise improvement of each finite inverse error.

2. The boundary input as a complete finite polynomial

First characterize the entire supported input in the second line of HM4. A distribution concentrated on r=0r=0r=0, localized in a compact tangential set, has finite normal order. Continuity on tests supported in a fixed compact neighborhood bounds its value by a constant times the largest derivative of total order at most some integer BBB: a neighborhood of zero in the test-function topology involves only finitely many such seminorms, and scaling gives that bound.
