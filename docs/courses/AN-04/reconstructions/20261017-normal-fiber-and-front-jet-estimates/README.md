# Normal-fiber bounds and finite front jets

Read [the proofs](normal-fiber-bounds-and-finite-front-jets.html) or edit [their source](normal-fiber-bounds-and-finite-front-jets.md). Two smooth output-coordinate derivatives suffice for a Hilbert-valued normal-frequency bound without frequency derivatives. A complete boundary residual also admits a finite exact front-jet operator whose difference, after a prescribed differential family, is bounded by the original Banach norm. Full cross-chart kernels, density factors, both side tails and compact physical projections are retained.

The prerequisite proof index gives the earlier results used here. Run `python -B model_check.py` and `python -B figures/build_figure.py` for the supporting algebra and diagram. The analytic proofs are in the lesson.

These bounds use their stated coordinate-smoothness and full residual front-jet hypotheses. For scalar tests on compact boundaries and the observation estimate, see [whole-manifold tangential-equator construction](../20261017-global-tangential-equator-assembly/one-scalar-test-when-all-boundary-tangential-directions-are-allowed.html) and [general scalar family and observation estimates](../20261017-angular-output-reserve-assembly/scalar-tests-from-a-missing-tangential-direction.html).
