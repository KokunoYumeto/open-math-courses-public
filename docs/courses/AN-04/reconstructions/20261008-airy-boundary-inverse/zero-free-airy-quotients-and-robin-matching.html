Zero-free Airy quotients and matrix Robin matching

The uniform normal estimate retains a homogeneous Robin boundary form whose sign is not controlled. A complementary boundary construction uses the complex Airy modes. Their logarithmic derivatives have no real zeros, whereas the corresponding real Airy quotient has poles. This lesson proves the zero-free statement, all differentiated real-axis estimates, the transition-frequency inverse bounds and the exact matrix Robin matching law for the affine Airy model.

The proof builds on the existing Airy lesson. Its entire Airy function, differential equation, positive Gaussian contour and real stationary-phase formula are retained. The new argument constructs the single outgoing mode by a convergent integral equation. It needs no unproved complex-sector asymptotic theorem.
