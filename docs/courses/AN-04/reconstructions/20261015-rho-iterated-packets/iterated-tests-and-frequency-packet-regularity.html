Iterated tests and frequency packet regularity

For ordinary symbols, the fixed dyadic L2L^2L2 endpoint and all iterated tangential tests recognize the precise Lagrangian order. For general symbols with 1/2<ρ<11/2<\rho<11/2<ρ<1, a frequency packet can occupy a much smaller portion of an annulus. Its annular L2L^2L2 size can stay fixed while its height grows. That distinction matters in an intrinsic criterion.

This lesson proves three statements. General-symbol operators of order 2ρ−12\rho-12ρ−1 whose principal symbols vanish on the Lagrangian preserve IρmI_\rho^mIρm​, so all their finite words satisfy the usual fixed endpoint. The reverse implication, with precisely the fixed endpoint and test class specified below, is false: a compactly supported packet sum gives a counterexample for every 1/2<ρ<11/2<\rho<11/2<ρ<1 and every real mmm. An exact criterion instead uses frequency cells of radius ⟨ξ⟩ρ\langle\xi\rangle^\rho⟨ξ⟩ρ and all weighted normal derivatives. We prove it in a frequency graph and then prove its intrinsic, microlocal meaning.

The primary antecedent is Lars Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators IV, Springer 2009 eBook, ISBN 978-3-642-00136-9, §25.1, Definition 25.1.1 and Proposition 25.1.3 (printed 4–6), and the proposed general-symbol intrinsic extension on printed 16.
