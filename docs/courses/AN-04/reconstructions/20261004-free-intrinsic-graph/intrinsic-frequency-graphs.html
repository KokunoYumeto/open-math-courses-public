Recovering a frequency amplitude from iterated regularity

An oscillation can conceal the amplitude that determines a distribution's order. In a frequency graph the concealed factor is explicit. Removing it turns suitable operators on the distribution into ordinary derivatives of the amplitude. Dyadic L2L^2L2 estimates then recover the exact symbol order.
