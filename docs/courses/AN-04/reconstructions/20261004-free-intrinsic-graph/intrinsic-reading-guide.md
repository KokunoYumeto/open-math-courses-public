# Intrinsic regularity: the current proof sequence

This private reading guide joins the completed local components.
The linked components and their exact prerequisite edition are checked and
synchronized in the private repository. The full course remains unfinished.

1. [Frequency amplitudes and iterated regularity](intrinsic-frequency-graphs.md)
   proves both directions of the exact graph criterion, retaining the
   endpoint Besov norm and finite-rank systems.
2. [Conic frequency coordinates](prerequisites/conic-frequency-coordinates.md)
   supplies the base-coordinate construction of a graph.
   [Coordinate and directional localization](prerequisites/coordinate-and-wavefront-localization.md)
   proves transport, wavefront covariance and proper conic cutoffs.
3. [Parametrices and intrinsic localization](prerequisites/conic-parametrices-and-localization.md)
   supplies the full elliptic-test converse and independence of
   sufficiently small cutoffs and Lagrangian extensions.
4. [Prescribed nondegenerate and clean phases](prerequisites/prescribed-phase-representation.md)
   supplies every ordinary-symbol remainder, the amplitude right inverse,
   the excess correction and the leading-symbol lower-order criterion.
5. [Sobolev improvement and the strict threshold](prerequisites/sobolev-order-refinement.md)
   supplies the separate Sobolev-to-symbol theorem, its fully localized
   counterexample and five worked checks of orders, systems, operator
   reordering and phase normalization.
6. [Phase signatures and the geometric Maslov line](prerequisites/relative-maslov-line.md)
   proves the reduced quadratic forms, signature comparison through
   rank changes, global line construction and phase-chart identification,
   with three complete examples.
7. [The global principal symbol](prerequisites/global-principal-symbol.md)
   proves the critical density transformation, all phase and Fourier
   normalization factors, the exact kernel and surjectivity.
   Its explicit partition has locally finite base supports, including
   on a noncompact manifold. Three solved examples check coordinate
   changes, stabilization and the point mass.

The linked analytic prerequisites give the actual earlier proofs of
measure and Fourier facts, dyadic estimates and ordinary operator
calculus. Each retains its own author and licence notices.

The tangent lesson
and its [Gaussian-symbol identification](../20261004-free-tangent-zoom/prerequisites/gaussian-symbol-line.md)
now complete the tangent comparison, including every Gaussian rank and the
nonlinear coordinate law. Their exact prerequisite edition is included.

The remaining course includes Fourier-integral composition, propagation, hyperbolic and boundary problems and complex phases. General symbol-class variants, topology/completeness and the separate involutivity statement are not certified by these ordinary-symbol components.
