## Conic inverses and full intrinsic localization — 4 October 2026

GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), Ultra, prepared this bounded modified selection of
AN03-U010 Section 2 and AN03-U012 Section 6 for the AN-04 local course
project. The original title, authorship, publisher and rights notices
are preserved. This component remains under GNU FDL Version 1.2 only,
without Invariant Sections or Cover Texts.

The selection is restricted to ordinary symbol summation and square
matrix conic inverses. The connecting proofs give every differentiated
cutoff estimate, compact proper operator summation, the finite left and
right correction identities on one fixed cone, finite conic Besov
reconstruction, all three parts of intrinsic localization, and
independence of sufficiently small cutoffs and Lagrangian extensions.
Every input has an exact earlier programme proof.

Human comparison uses Grubb's freely accessible author-hosted Chapter 7, Lemma 7.3, Theorem 7.18(1) and Corollary 7.19(1), and the freely readable paper Fourier integral operators. I, Propositions 1.1.9 and 2.5.1. Omitted external proofs and references are not imported. No human-source text or PDF is redistributed. The AN-03 originals are unchanged. Full phase representations and the remaining AN-04 course are unfinished.
