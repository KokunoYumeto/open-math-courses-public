Conic inverses and full intrinsic localization — 4 October 2026

The connecting proofs give every differentiated cutoff estimate, compact proper operator summation, the finite left and right correction identities on one fixed cone, finite conic Besov reconstruction, all three parts of intrinsic localization, and independence of sufficiently small cutoffs and Lagrangian extensions. Every input has an exact earlier programme proof.
