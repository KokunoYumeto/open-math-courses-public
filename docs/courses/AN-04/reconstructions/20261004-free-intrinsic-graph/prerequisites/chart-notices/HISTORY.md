## Elliptic Operators & Boundary Problems: Renewed 2026 Course Draft

The AN-03 course-writing task and OpenAI Codex rewrote and reorganized the 36 inherited units while preserving their mathematical objects, formulas, hypotheses, proofs and source identities. Those modified units remain under the same GNU Free Documentation License route. The renewed collection also adds separately licensed CC0 lessons. External books, papers and software are cited as antecedents or comparison sources. They are not included as source payloads and retain their own rights.

## Coordinate transport and directional localization — 4 October 2026

GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), Ultra, prepared this bounded modified selection of
AN03-U012 for the AN-04 local course project. Only the ordinary-symbol
coordinate formula, Fourier directional tests, nonstationary estimate,
diffeomorphism covariance and proper pseudolocality are selected. The
original title, authorship and rights notices above are preserved.
New connecting proofs supply finite-order test estimates, both Jacobians,
base-dependent regularization, essential-support transport, frame and
word invariance, compact conic cutoffs, and wavefront containment using a
fixed flat symbol test. Exact earlier programme proofs supply all inputs.

This modified component remains under GNU FDL Version 1.2 only, without
Invariant Sections or Cover Texts. Human-source comparison uses Grubb's
author-hosted Chapter 8, Section 8.1(1), and the freely readable 1971 paper
Fourier integral operators. I, Section 2.5. Neither human text nor PDF is
redistributed. The general conic parametrix and full intrinsic lesson
remain unfinished. The original AN-03 files are unchanged. Publication
is disabled.
