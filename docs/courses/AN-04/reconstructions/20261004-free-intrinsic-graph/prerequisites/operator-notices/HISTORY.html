Elliptic Operators & Boundary Problems: Renewed 2026 Course Draft

The AN-03 course-writing task and OpenAI Codex rewrote and reorganized the 36 inherited units while preserving their mathematical objects, formulas, hypotheses, proofs and source identities. Those modified units remain under the same GNU Free Documentation License route. The renewed collection also adds separately licensed CC0 lessons. The AN-03 local course project remains the publisher.

External books, papers and software are cited as antecedents or comparison sources. They are not included as source payloads and retain their own rights.
