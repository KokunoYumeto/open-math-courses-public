The intrinsic symbol as a family of tangent Gaussians

The tangent comparison becomes intrinsic once its coefficient is identified with the principal symbol and its Gaussian is allowed to depend on the second jet of the modulating phase. We prove that identification, including ordinary symbols without a fixed leading limit and Hessians of changing rank.
