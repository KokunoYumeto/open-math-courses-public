Variable matrix coefficients in the Airy boundary inverse

The zero-free Airy quotient gives an exact inverse for a boundary Fourier multiplier. A coefficient depending on the boundary point does not commute with that multiplier. This lesson proves the operator composition, Sobolev bounds and two-sided parametrices needed to handle that dependence. The reflection operator still has order zero, even though estimating its two factors separately would lose a third of a derivative.

We keep arbitrary finite complex matrices and their order of multiplication. The result applies to a boundary operator once its Fourier–Airy representation has actually been established. Constructing that representation from a general variable-coefficient differential equation, and identifying the same weak energy solution, remain further steps.
