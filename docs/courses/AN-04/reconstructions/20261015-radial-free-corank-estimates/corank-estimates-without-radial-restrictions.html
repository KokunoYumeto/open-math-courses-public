Corank estimates without radial restrictions

An ordinary canonical relation can have zero restricted canonical one-form. Its tangent relation still has a symplectic splitting, but a homogeneous change of coordinates cannot give its quotient a nonzero radial vector. We use ordinary affine symplectic coordinates on bounded frequency bands instead. Exact unitary quantizations make their estimates uniform in the band, and frequency orthogonality recombines the bands.

The primary antecedent is Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators IV, §25.3, the remark after Theorem 25.3.9, printed 32, in the approved Springer eBook, ISBN 978-3-642-00136-9. The independent argument below proves both estimates referred to in that remark. It is course exposition, not a transcription of the book.

The exact earlier proofs used here are the linear symplectic relation splitting, the diagonal-complement lemma, closed-form primitives, kernel action and axis estimates, uniform stationary phase, Fourier bounds, dyadic and Schur bounds, and proper Sobolev reconstruction. Their exact versions and individual locators are in the proof map. The 92 transitive human foundations remain explicitly external; internal export P514 closure is not claimed.
