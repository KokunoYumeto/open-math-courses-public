The weighted range estimate starts with coefficients already in their energy spaces. Here we start with an actual solution. Its weak interior Cauchy trace determines an incoming coefficient; an interior observation then proves the sharp regularity of that coefficient. The actual boundary value supplies the second coefficient. Their global hyperbolic assumptions are not silently imposed here.
