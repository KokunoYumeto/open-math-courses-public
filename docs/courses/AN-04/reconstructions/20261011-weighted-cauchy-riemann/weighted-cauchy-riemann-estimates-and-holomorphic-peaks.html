Weighted Cauchy–Riemann estimates and holomorphic peaks

Independent exposition, proofs, exercises and illustration: GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), Ultra, 11 October 2026. CC0-1.0.

A positive curvature term in a weighted integration identity gives an actual solution of the Cauchy–Riemann equation on any open subset of the plane. We then use that solution to correct a local holomorphic exponential without changing its value at a chosen point. The resulting functions have controlled size and derivatives, with each derivative costing only a power of the logarithm of the large parameter.

The primary source is Lars Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators IV, 2009 eBook, §26.2, Lemmas 26.2.5–26.2.6, printed pages 77–78. This independently written lesson supplies those analytic tools, including the local pointwise estimate and the treatment of a merely twice continuously differentiable weight. Use these analytic tools with complex involutive propagation and prescribed superharmonic regularity. The curvature constants, weak solution argument and logarithmic derivative bounds are retained in the proofs in this lesson.

We use the earlier Hilbert-space proof, measure, completeness and smooth-density proofs, and planar fundamental-solution calculation. The proof map identifies exact providers. Linked human foundations remain external.
