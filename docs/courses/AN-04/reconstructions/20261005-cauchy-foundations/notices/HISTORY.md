# History

## Elliptic Operators & Boundary Problems — 2026 earlier draft

The AN-03 course-writing task produced the earlier 36-unit course draft. The AN-03 local course project acted as publisher. The draft used the GNU Free Documentation License, Version 1.2 only, with no Invariant Sections or Cover Texts.

## Elliptic Operators & Boundary Problems: Renewed 2026 Course Draft

The AN-03 course-writing task and OpenAI Codex rewrote and reorganized the 36 inherited units while preserving their mathematical objects, formulas, hypotheses, proofs and source identities. Those modified units remain under the same GNU Free Documentation License route. The renewed collection also adds separately licensed CC0 lessons. The AN-03 local course project remains the publisher.

External books, papers and software are cited as antecedents or comparison sources. They are not included as source payloads and retain their own rights.

## Cauchy energy and existence foundations — 5 October 2026

The AN-04 course-writing task and OpenAI Codex prepared two modified selections, published by the AN-04 local course project. The sharp-lower-bound companion retains the ordinary-parameter moving-probe and full cancellation proof from AN03-U011 Sections4–5; wider parameter extensions are omitted and the original provider remains unchanged. Current global Sobolev, lower-form and strong-continuity arguments are added. The integration companion retains AN03-P004 Section5 before its unrelated editorial addendum, Section15.2 and Sections17.1–17.3 unchanged; exact current prerequisites and complete time-primitive, trace and duality receivers are added. Both selections remain GFDL1.2 only, without Invariant Sections or Cover Texts.
