Hilbert coefficients, ordered calculus and the sharp lower bound

The systems theorem needs estimates in operator norm. A bound obtained by summing matrix entries can grow with the matrix size and does not prove that theorem for an infinite-dimensional coefficient space. Here we prove the ordinary Hilbert-coefficient statements with constants depending on symbol seminorms and the spatial dimension alone.

The scalar inputs are the complete ordinary multiplier, composition and adjoint proofs O1–O3, the packet estimate E23–E27, and the moving-probe construction and every differentiated cancellation, G4–G5. The complete Hahn–Banach, Banach integration, Bochner-space, primitive and duality proofs supply the norm and integral operations. The Fourier proofs L1–L3, measure proofs M0–M8, and Hilbert representation proof T1 are also included earlier. Their component notices remain in place. This connecting exposition is independently written and dedicated to CC0 1.0.
