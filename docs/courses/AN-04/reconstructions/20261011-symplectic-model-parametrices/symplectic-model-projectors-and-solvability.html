Symplectic model projectors, parametrices and solvability

A right inverse fails when the forcing has a component in the adjoint kernel. A left inverse fails when a solution has a component in the kernel. For the finite-contact complex model these two failures live in opposite frequency directions. We construct the inverse and both projections, prove their exact wavefront relations and Sobolev bounds, and use the cokernel projection to decide microlocal solvability.

The mathematical antecedent is Lars Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators IV, 2009 edition, Section 26.3, printed pages 86–91: the even-contact fundamental solution, Propositions 26.3.7, 26.3.7′ and 26.3.8, and Theorem 26.3.9. The earlier finite-contact lesson, FCM:E0–F2 and N3, supplies exact coercivity, favorable microlocal regularity and the full variable-coefficient reduction. We give a direct one-dimensional proof of the inverse bounds instead of invoking an unproved continuity assertion about the complex kernels.
