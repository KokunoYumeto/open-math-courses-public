L2.2.1: Squeeze lemma

Let {an}n=1∞,\{ a_n \}_{n=1}^\infty\text{,}{an​}n=1∞​, {bn}n=1∞,\{ b_n \}_{n=1}^\infty\text{,}{bn​}n=1∞​, and {xn}n=1∞\{ x_n \}_{n=1}^\infty{xn​}n=1∞​ be sequences such that
