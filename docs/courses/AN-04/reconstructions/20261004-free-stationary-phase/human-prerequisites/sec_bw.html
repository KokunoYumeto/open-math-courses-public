L2.3.5: Convergence iff the two tail limits agree

Proposition 2.3.5.

Let {xn}n=1∞\{ x_n \}_{n=1}^\infty{xn​}n=1∞​ be a bounded sequence. Then {xn}n=1∞\{ x_n \}_{n=1}^\infty{xn​}n=1∞​ converges if and only if
lim inf⁡n→∞xn=lim sup⁡n→∞xn.\begin{equation*} \liminf_{n\to \infty} x_n = \limsup_{n\to \infty} x_n. \end{equation*}n→∞liminf​xn​=n→∞limsup​xn​.​
Furthermore, if {xn}n=1∞\{ x_n \}_{n=1}^\infty{xn​}n=1∞​ converges, then
lim⁡n→∞xn=lim inf⁡n→∞xn=lim sup⁡n→∞xn.\begin{equation*} \lim_{n\to \infty} x_n = \liminf_{n\to \infty} x_n = \limsup_{n\to \infty} x_n. \end{equation*}n→∞lim​xn​=n→∞liminf​xn​=n→∞limsup​xn​.​

Proof.

Let ana_nan​ and bnb_nbn​ be as in Definition 2.3.1. In particular, for all n∈N,n \in \N\text{,}n∈N,
bn≤xn≤an.\begin{equation*} b_n \leq x_n \leq a_n . \end{equation*}bn​≤xn​≤an​.​
First suppose lim inf⁡n→∞xn=lim sup⁡n→∞xn.\liminf_{n\to\infty} x_n = \limsup_{n\to\infty} x_n\text{.}liminfn→∞​xn​=limsupn→∞​xn​. Then {an}n=1∞\{ a_n \}_{n=1}^\infty{an​}n=1∞​ and {bn}n=1∞\{ b_n \}_{n=1}^\infty{bn​}n=1∞​ both converge to the same limit. By the squeeze lemma (Lemma 2.2.1), {xn}n=1∞\{ x_n \}_{n=1}^\infty{xn​}n=1∞​ converges and
lim⁡n→∞bn=lim⁡n→∞xn=lim⁡n→∞an.\begin{equation*} \lim_{n\to \infty} b_n = \lim_{n\to \infty} x_n = \lim_{n\to \infty} a_n . \end{equation*}n→∞lim​bn​=n→∞lim​xn​=n→∞lim​an​.​
Now suppose {xn}n=1∞\{ x_n \}_{n=1}^\infty{xn​}n=1∞​ converges to x.x\text{.}x. By Theorem 2.3.4, there exists a subsequence {xnk}k=1∞\{ x_{n_k} \}_{k=1}^\infty{xnk​​}k=1∞​ converging to lim sup⁡n→∞xn.\limsup_{n\to\infty} x_n\text{.}limsupn→∞​xn​. As {xn}n=1∞\{ x_n \}_{n=1}^\infty{xn​}n=1∞​ converges to x,x\text{,}x, every subsequence converges to xxx and so lim sup⁡n→∞xn=lim⁡k→∞xnk=x.\limsup_{n\to\infty} x_n = \lim_{k\to\infty} x_{n_k} = x\text{.}limsupn→∞​xn​=limk→∞​xnk​​=x. Similarly, lim inf⁡n→∞xn=x.\liminf_{n\to\infty} x_n = x\text{.}liminfn→∞​xn​=x.

Theorem 2.3.8. Bolzano–Weierstrass.

Suppose a sequence {xn}n=1∞\{ x_n \}_{n=1}^\infty{xn​}n=1∞​ of real numbers is bounded. Then there exists a convergent subsequence {xni}i=1∞.\{ x_{n_i} \}_{i=1}^\infty\text{.}{xni​​}i=1∞​.

Proof.

Theorem 2.3.4 says that there exists a subsequence whose limit is lim sup⁡n→∞xn.\limsup_{n\to\infty} x_n\text{.}limsupn→∞​xn​.
