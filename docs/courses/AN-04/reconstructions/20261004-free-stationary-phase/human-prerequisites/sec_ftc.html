L5.3.3: Primitive is Lipschitz; derivative equals f at every point of continuity, one-sided at endpoints.

Theorem 5.3.3.

Let f ⁣:[a,b]→Rf \colon [a,b] \to \Rf:[a,b]→R be a Riemann integrable function. Define
F(x)≔∫axf.\begin{equation*} F(x) \coloneqq \int_a^x f . \end{equation*}F(x):=∫ax​f.​
