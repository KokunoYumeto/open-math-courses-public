Linear algebra and differentiation

A real vector space has associative commutative addition, zero and additive inverses, and scalar multiplication satisfying the distributive, associative and unit axioms. Linear combinations are finite sums of scalar multiples. The span of a set consists of its finite linear combinations. A finite list is linearly independent if a linear combination equal to zero has all coefficients zero. A basis is an independent spanning list. The dimension is the size of a basis; the selected basis theorems justify that this does not depend on the basis.
