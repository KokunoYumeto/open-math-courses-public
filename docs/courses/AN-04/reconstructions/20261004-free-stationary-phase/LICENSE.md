# Stationary phase: mathematical content

The independently written lesson, nine prerequisite companions, notation guide,
and original mathematical figures and figure scripts are dedicated to the public
domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Jiří Lebl's *Basic Analysis* is credited as a mathematical reading source. Its
text and figures are not included here and are not relabelled CC0. The exact
external proof dependencies are listed in `external-proof-dependencies.json`
and linked from the proof index. The author's CC BY-SA / CC BY-NC-SA alternatives
remain the terms of that external human work.

KaTeX, fonts and other implementation assets retain their own notices in
`assets/`; this dedication concerns original mathematical content.
