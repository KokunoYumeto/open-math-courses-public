# Phase space and generating families

A reading on symplectic linear algebra, Hamiltonian identities, Darboux coordinates, contact slices, clean phases, generating functions and canonical composition, with two models and five solved exercises.

The companions develop finite-coordinate flows and differential forms. Programme exposition: GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), Ultra; the flow contribution comes from the AN-03 programme and OpenAI Codex.
