# Mathematical scope and sources

The phase-space reading develops the cotangent pairing, Hamiltonian signs, symplectic reduction, Darboux coordinates, dilation and contact slices, clean critical images, generating functions and canonical relations. It includes two models and five exercises with solutions.

The flow companion contains Sections 17.1–17.7 and equations NF1–NF21: local existence and uniqueness, parameter derivatives, inverse flows, continuation, composition and Jacobian determinants in the stated finite-dimensional norms. The form companion gives coordinate identities, radial homotopy and a common flow neighbourhood through a prescribed time interval.

Mathematical references are Guillemin–Sternberg, *Semi-classical Analysis*, author draft of 13 January 2010; Hörmander, *The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators III*, Springer eBook reprint 2007, ISBN 978-3-540-49938-1, of the corrected second printing 1994; and Hörmander, *Fourier integral operators. I*, Acta Mathematica 127 (1971), Section 3.1. These human works retain their own terms.
