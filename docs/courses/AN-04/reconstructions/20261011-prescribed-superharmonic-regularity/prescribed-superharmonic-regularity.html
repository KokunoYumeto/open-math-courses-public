Prescribing regularity on a complex characteristic leaf

The complex propagation theorem says that the Sobolev regularity index obeys superharmonic comparison along a nonradial involutive characteristic leaf. We now prove the converse for the planar model: every allowable superharmonic index occurs. The proof constructs smooth holomorphic packets that defeat each proposed stronger regularity estimate, then uses completeness to obtain one distribution satisfying all the required conditions simultaneously.

The mathematical antecedent is Lars Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators IV, 2009 edition, Section 26.2, Theorem 26.2.3 and Lemma 26.2.4, printed pages 76–80. The exposition and packet norm argument below are independently written. The earlier weighted planar lesson, WCR:P0–P2, proves the holomorphic peaks with every derivative bound. The complex propagation lesson, CIP:P0–P2 and N3, supplies the comparison foundations and complete scalar normal form. The current proof map also identifies exact ordinary-calculus, Fourier, Baire and complete-space providers. All uses retain their hypotheses and versions.
