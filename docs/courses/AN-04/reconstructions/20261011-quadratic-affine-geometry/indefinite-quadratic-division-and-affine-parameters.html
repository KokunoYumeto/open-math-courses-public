Indefinite quadratic division and affine characteristic parameters

A changing-rank characteristic can carry a preferred affine parameter even when its ordinary time parameter is not preferred. The construction needs more than a nonzero semidefinite Hessian. We prove exact division when the transverse imaginary Hessian is indefinite, derive its invariant transport, and construct affine charts after collapsing intervals on which that Hessian vanishes. Ordinary compact tubes make the argument apply to radial characteristics as well.

The antecedent is Lars Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators IV, Springer 2009 eBook, ISBN 978-3-642-00136-9, Section 26.5, printed 114–116. We supply an independent proof of the indefinite case of Proposition 26.5.7 and of the affine construction on the indefinite extension. The full semidefinite remainder assertion is proved in the nonnegative-quotient companion. CPG:X3 already gives an explicit obstruction to extending Hessian proportionality to every semidefinite characteristic.

The proof map records exact earlier versions and locators. PSG supplies compact ordinary tubes and the all-multiplier sign criterion. FN supplies complete smooth complex division. GGL supplies parameter Morse coordinates and smooth even/odd extension. Human foundations remain external.
