A fixed interior observation test for transverse reflection

The uniform reflection estimate used the norm of an entire transported branch on an interior strip. Here we replace that norm by one fixed ordinary pseudodifferential observation of the original solution, supported near the observed characteristic graph. The complementary normal frequencies are controlled by the actual source and the coarse mixed norm. The observation test is independent of the operator parameter.

The antecedent is Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators III, 2007 edition, the ordinary/tangential comparison of §18.1, the reflection argument of Theorem 24.2.1 and the parameter requirement in Proposition 24.7.1′. We give the complete quantitative reduction, including a calculus whose remainders improve tangential order without being called normally smoothing. The proof uses the programme's ordinary calculus, mixed composition proof, finite-derivative boundedness theorem and the exact current reflection factors.
