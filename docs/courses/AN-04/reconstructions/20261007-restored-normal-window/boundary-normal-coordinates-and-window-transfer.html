Boundary normal coordinates and quadratic window transfer

A normal form is useful only if its coordinates preserve the regularity that the analytic estimate measures. A time-dependent change of spatial coordinates can remove the normal cross term, while introducing a tangential mixed time term. We retain that term and transport the wavefront itself. This connects the glancing estimate to the geometric normal form without applying the estimate to a different differential operator.

The chapter proves the exact transfer for the entire smooth single-face coefficient class of The compensated glancing estimate and Smooth glancing regularity in parabolic windows. The cross block is required to vanish at the boundary; it need not vanish inside the collar. The resulting geometric quadratic-window criterion is the input RW4 of Parabolic wavefront windows and singular rays. Its use in the full trajectory theorem still requires the matching propagation statements on the non-glancing pieces.
