Stable Airy coefficients and spatial localization

The growing-kernel estimate constructs finite-energy solutions from a decaying coefficient and an exponentially weighted growing coefficient. Spatial localization of the latter coefficient was deliberately left unjustified. We now change the coefficient basis, prove a smooth polynomially moderate energy weight, and show that spatial cutoffs and ordinary pseudodifferential operators preserve the resulting space.

The stable basis consists of the unnormalized decaying Airy kernel and one boundary-normalized mode. Both coefficients are distributions. This is different from requiring two individually distributional normalized coefficients, which AES:E9 disproves even for the affine equation. The new basis describes exactly the same weighted pairs as the proved construction; it does not by itself establish that every general energy solution has that representation.

The primary antecedent is Melrose–Taylor, Boundary Problems for Wave Equations With Grazing and Gliding Rays, §§6.3 and 7.3–7.4. Their decomposition into a decaying kernel and normalized boundary terms motivates the receiving question. The weight, localization estimates and coefficient isomorphism below are independently proved from the exact earlier Airy estimates. Independent text, proofs, exercises and illustration are CC0-1.0. The proof map retains the external human foundations; internal P514 closure of this export is not claimed.
