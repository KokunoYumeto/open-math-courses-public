Fixed source tests by packing residual outputs

Original exposition, proofs, exercises and illustration: GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), Ultra, 9 October 2026. CC0-1.0. The antecedent is Lars Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators III, corrected 1994 edition, Springer 2007 eBook, Sections 18.3 and 24.7. The argument below supplies a constructive fixed-test step in the parameter-dependent observation problem.

The admissible regularization proof controls a regularized solution by finitely many source operators that can vary with the coefficient parameter. Here we replace a smooth compact parameter family of those operators by two fixed tests: one elliptic in the prescribed compressed cone and one residual. The residual test is an actual compact boundary operator; its source contribution is not discarded.

We use the complete resolved kernels and boundary calculus, uniform microsupport and separated products, periodic Fourier basis and Parseval proof, complete smooth test spaces, and L2 integration and duality. Their exact transitive Lebl proofs remain external author-edition providers.

2. Turn smooth parameter dependence into rapidly decreasing coefficients

S1. Compact parameter localization. First suppose ZZZ lies in a cube in an open parameter neighborhood. Rescale that cube into the interior of (−π,π)p(-\pi,\pi)^p(−π,π)p, multiply the family by a parameter cutoff equal to one on ZZZ, and extend by zero periodically. This produces a smooth family on the parameter torus. Its operator supports stay fixed, and every complete residual seminorm of every parameter derivative is bounded.

For a general compact parameter set, take a finite partition of unity subordinate to such cubes. Each localized piece is extended separately. At any original parameter the original family is the finite sum of its localized pieces, evaluated in their own torus coordinates. It therefore suffices to dominate the sum of the suprema of this enlarged finite collection. Constant families can be regarded as families on one dummy circle; this also covers parameter dimension zero.
