At the other glancing points the proved parabolic-window estimate supplies singular points with the correct first-order direction. The exact components are the normal-coordinate window transfer, interior theorem, reflection bridge, both-domain strict diffraction, parabolic-window bridge and generalized-flow theorem. 8. The union theorem and its quantifiers

T7. Complete the receiving theorem. Every constructed ray lies in FFF, and T5–T6 construct one through every point of FFF. Taking their union proves the theorem in T0. All statements are for the full vector front; the lower matrix terms may move singularity between components.

Suppose now that the region has no infinite-contact points. GGL:T025–T026 proves local finite-contact uniqueness; ordinary, transverse, diffractive and strict gliding uniqueness cover the other points. With the fixed clock, two rays through the same state coincide wherever both are defined, by continuing that local uniqueness across their common interval. The singular ray from T6 must therefore be the unique maximal geometric ray in OOO. If the latter extended farther within OOO, its finite endpoint limit would contradict the escape just proved. Hence the whole unique ray is singular.
