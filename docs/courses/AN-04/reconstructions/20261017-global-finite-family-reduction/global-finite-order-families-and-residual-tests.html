Global finite-order families and residual tests

Complete boundary residual kernels may connect different coordinate charts and boundary components. Their compact input and output projections do not require the entire boundary to be compact. We extend the scalar residual factor to this setting and combine it with the full global parametrix. The result is a reduction of an arbitrary bounded finite-order family to two scalar norms, retaining the original Banach remainder.

The first scalar test is positive and elliptic on the prescribed compact conic microsupport. The second is a complete residual test. The statement below keeps those two norms explicit. It supplies the full global family reduction needed before a separate scalar gluing argument; it does not identify a sum of squares with a norm that dominates both factors.
