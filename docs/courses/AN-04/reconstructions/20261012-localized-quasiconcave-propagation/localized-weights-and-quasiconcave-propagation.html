Localized weights and quasi-concave propagation

A global solution estimate measures the entire forcing. Propagation needs a forcing measurement in a smaller phase-space region. Complex transported weights supply that localization: their real parts are small where regularity is wanted and large where a cutoff changes. We prove the complete localized estimate, then use frequency shells to recover the Sobolev index on every one-dimensional characteristic interval.

The antecedents are Lars Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators IV, admitted 2009 edition, Proposition 26.10.4, Lemma 26.10.5 and Theorem 26.10.1 with its compact reformulation, printed pages 149 and 155–160. The adaptive estimate, APE:H0–E2, supplies the global estimate and retained energy. The uniform barrier proof, DAQ:B0–B2, supplies the actual complex solutions used below. The proof map pins every earlier result.

The general theorem uses constant comparisons and requires no affine parameter. The corrected semidefinite clock from SCP is retained where its earlier theorem is used. We do not infer global quasi-concavity from local quasi-concavity.
