Distributional detail: no hidden term at the corner

If g∈L1(R2)g\in L^1(\mathbb R^2)g∈L1(R2), its Fourier transform tends to zero at infinity. Its Fourier transform is a polynomial by (SC1), and a polynomial tending to zero is identically zero: restriction to every ray kills its top homogeneous part, and descending through its degrees kills them all.
