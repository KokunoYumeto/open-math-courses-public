Strict Sobolev loss and compact solvability

A regularity statement about compactly supported adjoint distributions can yield solutions near a compact set. When regularity is stated as a Sobolev index, its endpoint need not be attained. We retain that distinction throughout the duality argument. We also construct one smooth solution for smooth data by a convergent correction in every smooth seminorm.

The mathematical antecedent is Hörmander IV, Theorem 26.11.3 and its reference to the proof of Theorem 26.1.7, printed pages 162–163 and 63–64 of the approved 2009 eBook, ISBN 978-3-642-00136-9. The earlier compact-support lesson supplies its complete functional prerequisites. Here the principal symbol may be complex, and the adjoint regularity hypothesis has an arbitrarily small strict loss.

The analytic transfer is proved under the explicit hypothesis below. Escape regularity proves the adjoint gain assumed here from condition (P) and the full two-ended escape geometry. The trapping criterion gives the equivalent characteristic formulation. The finite Sobolev loss remains strict, and the smooth argument constructs one solution. The escape-stability lemma in Section 2 is proved here.
