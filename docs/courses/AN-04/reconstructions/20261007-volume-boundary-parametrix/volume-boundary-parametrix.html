Recovering a collar solution from its forcing and boundary data

The inverse on a doubled collar, its boundary layer and the inverse of the boundary measurements now fit into one exact construction. The errors matter: one comes from the volume equation, another from the Cauchy projection, and a cancellation gives an additional derivative in the measured trace of volume data. We prove every identity on its actual domain, then return to the original collar with the normal cutoff terms present.

This independent exposition is CC0. The mathematical antecedent is the edition of Lars Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators III, Springer 2007, ISBN 978-3-540-49938-1, Section 20.1. The existing AN-03 Fredholm boundary problems with first-order Calderón defects, Sections 1–7, supplied the comparison of the volume and boundary identities. Its actual proofs have been checked against the earlier AN-04 constructions cited below. Linked components retain their own terms.
