A characteristic covector at a tangency has zero normal momentum. We need a corresponding estimate for a localized weak solution, with the source and all lower-order terms retained. The exact Fourier model established the mechanism when coefficients depended only on the normal variable. Here coefficients may depend on every base variable. The result applies to a scalar wave principal form acting on a fixed finite-dimensional vector space, with complex matrix lower-order terms and either Dirichlet or weak Neumann data.
