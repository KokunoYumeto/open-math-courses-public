A flat convex boundary with two characteristic continuations

A smooth convex obstacle can have infinitely many transverse reflections in a finite travel time. At their accumulation point the ordinary ray has contact of every order with the boundary. The reflected continuation and the boundary gliding continuation then pass through the same full characteristic state.

We construct the boundary, the polygon, its exact reflection law and both Hamilton lifts. The construction complements the variable-coefficient example in Tangency, gliding and generalized characteristic motion, Section 10. Its inputs are the contact-order calculation in that lesson's Section 2, its generalized-arc definition in Section 3, and ordinary smooth calculus and convergent series in the stationary-phase foundations. The exact earlier proofs are identified in the proof map.

Hörmander III, Springer 2007 edition, ISBN 978-3-540-49938-1, Example 24.3.11, printed 438, gives the convex-boundary nonuniqueness phenomenon. The formulas and interpolation below are independently chosen. The book is a valid approved source; its pages are not reproduced.
