Finite contact: complex models, regularity and isolated singularities

A complex characteristic can behave differently in the two directions of a covector ray. In one direction the equation forces extra Sobolev regularity. In the other, a decaying transverse solution can carry a singularity at a single point. We prove both conclusions, including the full operator reduction that makes the model apply to variable coefficients.

The mathematical antecedent is Lars Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators IV, admitted 2009 edition, Section 26.3, Proposition 26.3.1 through Theorem 26.3.6, printed pages 81–86. The geometric reductions are already proved in real and complex normal forms, FN:S2 and K0–K4. The graph-operator lesson, GE:D7–D11, supplies full Sobolev continuity, graph inverses and Egorov reduction. Exact Fourier, measure, ordinary-symbol and compactness providers are identified in the proof map.
