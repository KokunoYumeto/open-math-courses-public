Why local solvability requires condition Ψ

Solvability gives an estimate for test functions in terms of their adjoint images. We construct test functions concentrated near a forbidden sign change whose adjoint images are too small for that estimate to hold. The construction works when the sign change is infinitely flat or separated by a whole interval of zeros.

The mathematical antecedent is Lars Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators IV, admitted 2009 edition, Section 26.4, Theorem 26.4.7 and its reduced form, Corollary 26.4.8, and Lemmas 26.4.14–26.4.16, printed pages 96 and 102–110. The proof below uses the established condition Ψ geometry, PSG:F1, M0–M1, E1 and N0–N2; the exact solvability estimate, LSE:D0–D2; and the complex nonstationary proof, PI:CS0. The ordinary and graph calculus, finite-dimensional flows and smooth division are linked through the exact proof map.
