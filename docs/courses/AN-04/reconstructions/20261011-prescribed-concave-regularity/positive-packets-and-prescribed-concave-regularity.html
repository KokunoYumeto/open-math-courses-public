Positive Fourier packets with a prescribed concave regularity index

A solution can have exactly one singular covector direction at each point of a line, while its Sobolev regularity changes along that line. We construct such solutions with any finite concave index allowed by the sign of a quadratic form. The construction is an actual convergent positive sum of exact solutions. It proves that no cancellation can hide a required singularity.

The antecedent is Hörmander IV, Example 26.6.5, printed 121–123 in the admitted 2009 edition. We retain its logarithmically shifted packets, derive their full Fourier and Sobolev bounds, and give a direct positive-series realization of the concave envelope. The earlier prescribed superharmonic lesson supplies a related complete-space construction; positivity here permits a simpler explicit selection of coefficients. The ordinary propagation proof explains the sign restriction when the quadratic form is nonnegative. Exact Fourier, distribution, localization and calculus providers appear in the proof map.
