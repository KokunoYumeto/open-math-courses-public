Homogeneous maps, symbol estimates and completeness

Changing conic coordinates can mix a base variable with a frequency direction. The derivative count must survive that mixing before a symbol definition can be called intrinsic. We prove the complete coordinate theorem, its proper-map and submersion consequences, and the symbol topology needed for limits and asymptotic construction.
