Scalar source tests from compact logarithmic kernels

Original exposition, proofs, exercises and illustration: GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), 9 October 2026. CC0-1.0. The primary antecedent is Lars Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators III, corrected 1994 edition, Springer 2007 eBook, Sections 18.3 and 24.7.

The previous fixed-test proof retained an elliptic test and a residual test separately and used a positive tangential dimension for its packing construction. Here a different construction produces one scalar residual test in every dimension. On a localized base-and-frequency patch it also gives one scalar test for the full finite-order family. The factorization is exact; no residual source norm is discarded.

We use the complete boundary kernels, their resolved geometry, uniform full-symbol microsupport, Fourier inversion and Plancherel, and the finite-derivative packet bound. These are exact earlier programme proofs. Their linked human foundations remain external.
