A uniform half-order gain at general glancing

An estimate for each operator does not give an estimate for a family of operators. The constants, coordinate neighborhoods and actual boundary traces must be controlled together. We prove that control for the general scalar glancing estimate, at every real starting Sobolev order. One geometric neighborhood works throughout a compact smooth parameter family and through arbitrarily many finite regularity steps.

This is the local analytic input to the parameter theorem of Hörmander, The Analysis of Linear Partial Differential Operators III, corrected edition reprinted in 2007, Proposition 24.7.1′, printed page 467. The underlying commutant is the one used in Proposition 24.5.1 and Lemma 24.5.2, printed pages 455–458. We give the coefficient-uniform argument, including the characteristic completion and boundary pairings. The final observation estimate in an arbitrary admissible Banach norm still requires its global graph argument; it is not asserted here.

The fixed-operator scalar proof, finite-order trace and comparison proof, smooth characteristic completion and uniform damping calculation supply exact earlier arguments. The proof map binds their current versions. Lebl foundations remain external.
