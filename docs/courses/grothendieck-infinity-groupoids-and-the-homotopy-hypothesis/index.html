Grothendieck infinity-groupoids and the homotopy hypothesis

Henry's pushout conjecture for every Grothendieck coherator, after OpenAI's 2026 proof: freely adjoining to a cellular infinity-groupoid a cell parallel to a given one, and a cell joining the two, is a weak equivalence. Globular theories and their models; the homotopy calculus; coskeletal cutoffs, spines and free shapes; trees and whiskers; partial cylinders and the main theorem; the canonical semi-model structure and the Quillen equivalence with spaces.

Six lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  2. The homotopy calculus
  3. Cutoffs, spines and free shapes
  4. Trees and whiskers
  5. Partial cylinders and the main theorem
  6. The semi-model structure and the homotopy hypothesis
