Cotangent complexes and deformations in ringed topoi

Homotopical algebra in a ringed topos and the cohomology of diagrams, as foundations for the cotangent complex of morphisms of ringed topoi: homotopy sheaves and the free abelian Whitehead theorem; derived functors of non-additive functors of complexes, the Deligne-Lazard theorem and the shift formulas; décalage, nerves of internal categories and the homotopy type of a category; strict simplicial objects and Deligne's contractibility theorem; derived colimits; fibred topoi, their total topoi, direct images and modules.

Eight lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  1. Homotopy sheaves and the free abelian Whitehead theorem
  2. Non-additive functors of complexes in a ringed topos
  3. Koszul complexes and the shift formulas
  4. Décalage, nerves and the homotopy type of a category
  5. Strict simplicial objects and Deligne's contractibility theorem
  6. Derived colimits over a small category
  7. Fibred topoi and their total topoi
  8. Morphisms of fibred topoi and modules on total topoi
