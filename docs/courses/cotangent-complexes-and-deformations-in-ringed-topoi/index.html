Cotangent complexes and deformations in ringed topoi

Homotopical algebra in a ringed topos and the cohomology of diagrams, as foundations for the cotangent complex of morphisms of ringed topoi: homotopy sheaves and the free abelian Whitehead theorem; derived functors of non-additive functors of complexes, the Deligne-Lazard theorem and the shift formulas; décalage, nerves of internal categories and the homotopy type of a category; strict simplicial objects and Deligne's contractibility theorem; derived colimits; fibred topoi, their total topoi, direct images and modules; the cohomology of total topoi and of simplicial topoi, and cohomological descent; the second total topos and local systems; left derived functors of left adjoints; a duality formula for diagrams; equivariant sheaves on simplicial topoi, the nerves of induced and coinduced modules, and the cohomology and Ext groups of equivariant modules; spectral diagrams, the stabilization of non-additive functors and stable derived functors; simplicial rings and modules, their calculus of fractions, triangles, Ext groups, derived tensor products and their spectral sequences; differential graded modules in a topos and their comparison with simplicial modules.

26 lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.
