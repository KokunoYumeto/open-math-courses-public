Symplectic balls and the symmetric Mahler inequality

OpenAI's theorem (2026) that for every origin-symmetric convex body K in R^n, n >= 2, every symplectic ball of capacity c < 4 embeds into int K x int K°, and its consequence, the symmetric Mahler inequality vol(K) vol(K°) >= 4^n/n!. Polar bodies, support functions and strip bodies; one- and two-forms, the Pfaffian and volume preservation, flows and Moser's method; plurisubharmonic potentials and dd^c; a ball of capacity below pi k from a holomorphic zero of order k; a planar conformal map from the arctangent series and a uniform estimate for its horizontal slice integrals; a symplectic realization inside int K x S int K° with S close to pi k/4, rescaling, and polar approximation of general bodies.

Seven lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  1. Polar bodies and the volume product
  2. Forms, flows and Moser's method
  3. Plurisubharmonic potentials
  4. A ball from a holomorphic zero
  5. A planar conformal map
  6. The uniform slice estimate
  7. Realization in a polar product

Prerequisites

  • Local tools for bundles and transport (Bundles, curvature and holonomy)
  • Linear algebra and multivariable calculus, with the inverse and implicit function theorems, as in the core course Real Analysis II (Lebl, Theorems 8.5.1 and 8.5.6).
  • Complex analysis of one variable, as in the core course Complex Analysis (Lebl's guide, cited by number).
  • Lebesgue measure, dominated convergence, differentiation under the integral sign, Fubini's theorem and the change of variables formula, as in the core course Measure and Integration (Fremlin, cited by number).
  • Local solutions of ordinary differential equations and their smooth dependence on initial data: Theorem 2.1 of the lesson Local tools for bundles and transport of the course Bundles, curvature and holonomy; the inverse function theorem, Theorem 1.2 there.
