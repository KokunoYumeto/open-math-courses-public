Marked Type II estimates for prime predecessors

OpenAI's one-sided marked Type II estimate for prime predecessors: bilinear sums of the prime indicator minus its rough-number average against arbitrary rough coefficients, at the predecessors mn - 1 and weighted by marks from fixed bands of primes, are smaller than every power of log x. A coefficient criterion; a zero-free strip at large height and long prime polynomials; Cauchy's inequality and a determinant equation; physical states, root residues and independent lines; lifespans, a memory of pending primes and contraction on clean and dirty edges; birth distinctness; the major arcs by characters, Mellin separation and sparse frequencies.

17 lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.
