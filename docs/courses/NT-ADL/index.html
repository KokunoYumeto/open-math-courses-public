  8. Tate's local theory at the infinite places
  9. Tate's global theory: continuation and functional equation
  10. Hecke L-functions and the Dedekind zeta function
  11. Lattices over a local field: the tree of GL₂ and its decompositions
  12. The spherical Hecke algebra of GL₂ and Hecke operators on lattices
