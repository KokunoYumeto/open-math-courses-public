Modularity of elliptic curves over imaginary quadratic fields

Every elliptic curve over every imaginary quadratic field is modular, with matching local parameters at every place, after OpenAI's 2026 manuscript. Cyclic covers of a twist branched at two points; integral homology over the group ring and Picard-Lefschetz transvections; congruences between character pieces; local constancy of reduction types; Hilbert irreducibility for a curve and its conjugate; three applications of the Caraiani-Newton lifting theorem; cyclic descent; complex multiplication.

Eight lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  1. Elliptic curves over imaginary quadratic fields and their automorphy
  2. Cyclic covers of an elliptic curve with two branch points
  3. Two degenerations and a Jacobian of compact type
  4. Integral homology and Picard–Lefschetz transvections
  5. Rank-two systems and their congruences
  6. Prescribing the local reduction type
  7. Residual irreducibility and one specialization
  8. Three lifts and descent
