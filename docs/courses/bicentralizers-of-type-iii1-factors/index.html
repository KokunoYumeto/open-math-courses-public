Bicentralizers of type III₁ factors

Bicentralizers of type III₁ factors and Connes' bicentralizer problem. Connes–Størmer transitivity makes the asymptotic centralizer of every faithful normal state a II₁ factor; the bicentralizer, its description in the Ocneanu ultrapower, its state-preserving expectation and the Houdayer–Isono self-bicentralizing property; Connes' isomorphism and the bicentralizer flow, built in a single ultrapower; modular averaging, bounded recovery and spectral intertwining rigidity (OpenAI, 2026), which make the flow trivial; Haagerup's criterion for a trivial bicentralizer; Marrakchi's strongly invariant states; and the theorem that every type III₁ factor with separable predual has trivial bicentralizer.

Eight lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  1. Asymptotic centralizers of type III₁ factors
  2. The bicentralizer
  3. The bicentralizer flow
  4. Modular averaging and bounded recovery
  5. Spectral intertwining rigidity
  6. Haagerup's criterion for a trivial bicentralizer
  7. Strongly invariant states and approximate eigenstates
  8. The bicentralizer of a type III₁ factor is trivial
