Artin's primitive root conjecture

Every integer other than -1 that is not a square is a primitive root modulo at least c x/(log x)^2 primes in (x, 2x), after OpenAI's 2026 paper. A zero-free strip Re s > 1 - 10^-6 for the Hecke L-functions of every cyclotomic field containing the twelfth roots of unity, from cubic Gauss sums: S-integers, sextic reciprocity, Kummer characters, the cubic theta function of Kazhdan and Patterson, a Weyl reflection, a quadratic large sieve, a Poisson probe and its Euler product, zero detection; uniform complete splitting in Kummer fields; primes p = crQ + 1 from a block sieve, Bombieri-Vinogradov, marked bilinear estimates and Buchstab's densities.

24 lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.
