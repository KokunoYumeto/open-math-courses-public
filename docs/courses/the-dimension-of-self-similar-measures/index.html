The dimension of self-similar measures

Entropy of random variables with finitely many values: the chain rule, conditioning, mutual information, mixtures, and estimates for the binary entropy. Entropy of a law on the line at a scale, averaged over the positions of the grid: comparison of scales, concavity, convolution, uniform steps. Self-similar measures on the line; entropy at small scales and dimension; uniform nonsaturation. The two-point gain; pair capacities; the gain of a convolution from fair pairs. The pair bound for a finite law, by random nested partitions. The entropy rate of the walk of maps; the upper bound for the dimension. Block types; entropy of translations given the types; pair capacity at fine scales. The dimension formula d = min(1, h/chi); the formula without exact overlaps. The course is an exposition, with a complete proof, of OpenAI's theorem (2026) on the dimension of self-similar measures on the line; the proof assumes that the measure is exact dimensional.

Eight lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  1. Entropy of finite random variables
  2. Entropy at a scale
  3. The one-bit deficit of a self-similar measure
  4. The gain from a pair of points
  5. The pair bound for a finite law
  6. The random walk of maps and the upper bound for the dimension
  7. Block types and pairs at fine scales
  8. Disjoint windows and the dimension formula
