Smooth approximation with the exact Gauss constraint

Gauss’s law restricts the initial electric field. Cutting off or smoothing that field usually changes its covariant divergence. This chapter constructs an elliptic correction that restores the constraint exactly. The construction works for the actual connection in L6L^6 with gradient in L2L^2; it does not require a global L3L^3 bound. When its curvature also belongs to L2L^2, the approximations converge in the full physical energy.

Read initial data and energy and the first constrained approximation. The earlier projection is recovered on its original domain. The larger domain below is needed to use the initial-data stability theorem with the course’s homogeneous connection space.

The arrows are the actual operations GA.44–GA.48. The last arrow changes only the electric field. The error estimates and curvature condition are proved in GA.49–GA.55.

3. Existence and exact properties of the correcting map

For f∈L2(ℝ3;𝔤3)f\in L^2(\mathbb R^3;\mathfrak g^3), the functional

rA,f(ψ)=−∫∑iκ(fi,DiAψ) r_{A,f}(\psi)=-\int\sum_i\kappa(f_i,D_i^A\psi) \tag{GA.16}
(GA.16)

is continuous on 𝒱A\mathcal V_A, with dual norm at most ∥f∥2\|f\|_2. There exists a unique ϕA[f]∈𝒱A\phi_A[f]\in\mathcal V_A such that

bA(ϕA[f],ψ)=rA,f(ψ)(ψ∈𝒱A). b_A(\phi_A[f],\psi)=r_{A,f}(\psi) \quad(\psi\in\mathcal V_A). \tag{GA.17}
(GA.17)

A minimizing sequence is Cauchy by the parallelogram identity, because the average of two members remains in that set. Completeness gives a minimizer ψ*\psi_*. For h∈ker⁡rA,fh\in\ker r_{A,f}, minimality of ψ*+th\psi_*+th for every real tt gives bA(ψ*,h)=0b_A(\psi_*,h)=0.
