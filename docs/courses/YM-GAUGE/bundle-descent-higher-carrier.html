Bundle descent and the higher carrier

Lesson 3 · Geometry and states in Yang–Mills theory

The colour map of Lesson 1 and the compactly supported fields of Lesson 2 were written in a chosen quaternionic frame. We now follow them through a change of frame, construct their global bundles, and place those bundles over the higher carrier F4/ρ(Sp⁡(1))F_4/\rho(\operatorname{Sp}(1)). The final construction is a connection on the parameter space times spacetime. Its parameter curvature and mixed curvature are calculated explicitly.

Use Lesson 1 for quaternion multiplication, the matrix map φ\varphi, and the complete quadratic-map fibres and ranks. Use Lesson 2 for the exact three spatial curls and their complete curvature, source and energy formulas.

Two earlier geometric results enter here as exact programme providers. Their content and use are specified so that the dependency can be followed. First, the retained source constructs the based space X≅S6X\cong S^6, its specified map H=p3q2:X→S4H=p_3q_2:X\to S^4, and a based diffeomorphism d:S6→Xd:S^6\to X, and proves

The complete provider is the retained source, theorem ret:main and its proof. Second, its ordered-frame stabilizer and tangent construction identifies the compact group G=Spin⁡(8)G=\operatorname{Spin}(8) as the stabilizer of an ordered Jordan frame in L=F4L=F_4, with dimensions 2828 and 5252. The tangent formulas used below are proved at the same provider’s labels att:eq:D-table through att:eq:bundle-comparison, beginning at its explicit tangent construction. 1. A transition function that can be checked directly

8. A connection that includes parameter directions

Use the original faithful action of LL on the real Albert algebra JJ. The bilinear form on its Lie algebra is

𝔟(U,V)=−Tr⁡J(UV). \mathfrak b(U,V)=-\operatorname{Tr}_J(UV). \tag{3.44}
(3.44)

It is positive definite: the action preserves the Albert trace metric, so UU is skew-adjoint and −Tr⁡J(U2)=Tr⁡J(U*U)>0-\operatorname{Tr}_J(U^2)=\operatorname{Tr}_J(U^*U)>0 for U≠0U\ne0. Faithfulness rules out a nonzero acting derivation with zero matrix. Cyclicity of the trace proves invariance under conjugation. No scale factor is inserted into (3.44).

Let 𝔫=𝔨⟂𝔟\mathfrak n=\mathfrak k^{\perp_{\mathfrak b}} and split the left Maurer–Cartan form:

αL=ℓ−1dℓ=ω+θ,ω=pr⁡𝔨αL,θ=pr⁡𝔫αL. \alpha_L=\ell^{-1}d\ell=\omega+\theta,\qquad \omega=\operatorname{pr}_{\mathfrak k}\alpha_L,\qquad \theta=\operatorname{pr}_{\mathfrak n}\alpha_L . \tag{3.45}
(3.45)

Invariance of 𝔟\mathfrak b shows that 𝔫\mathfrak n is Ad⁡(K)\operatorname{Ad}(K)-invariant. Explicitly, for Z,V∈𝔨Z,V\in\mathfrak k and W∈𝔫W\in\mathfrak n, 𝔟([Z,W],V)=−𝔟(W,[Z,V])=0\mathfrak b([Z,W],V)=-\mathfrak b(W,[Z,V])=0. Thus [𝔨,𝔫]⊂𝔫[\mathfrak k,\mathfrak n]\subset\mathfrak n, and the two projections commute with Ad⁡(K)\operatorname{Ad}(K).

A principal connection is a Lie-algebra-valued one-form returning ZZ on the vertical fundamental vector ddt|0ℓexp⁡(tZ)\frac{d}{dt}|_0\ell\exp(tZ), and satisfying Rk*ω=Ad⁡k−1ωR_k^*\omega=\operatorname{Ad}_{k^{-1}}\omega. Formula (3.45) has both properties: the Maurer–Cartan form returns ZZ on that vector and transforms by the stated conjugation. Projection preserves these identities.
