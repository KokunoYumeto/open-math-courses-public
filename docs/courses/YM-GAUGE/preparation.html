Preparation

Start with basic algebra, elementary calculus and matrix arithmetic. The first lessons review fields, coordinates, derivatives, physical quantities and electromagnetism. Matrix groups and their Lie algebras follow, before connections and bundles.

Geometry, Fourier and Sobolev analysis, Hilbert spaces and quantum mechanics enter through staged prerequisite components. Their exact provider statements and proofs must match the receiving lesson. No workbench construction is an entry prerequisite.

The full plan gives learning outcomes and the subject sequence. The eighteen lessons are complete. Begin with fields, coordinates and physical quantities. Lesson 3 supplies the matrix and complex-number preparation and reuses the complete RT-LIE commutator argument with exact attribution. Lesson 4 constructs the local matrix-group charts and Lie-algebra maps, with exact comparisons to Etingof and the compact-group course. Lesson 5 supplies the required local exterior calculus and connection formulas, with exact DG-FND comparisons. Lesson 6 supplies complete bundle, transport and holonomy constructions with exact geometry-source comparisons. Lesson 7 proves the metric and variational prerequisites for the action, its field equations, boundary terms and scalar matter coupling. Lesson 8 derives the initial-data constraints, constructs temporal gauge, and proves stress-energy, local flux and total energy identities. Lesson 9 supplies Fourier and Sobolev preparation and complete classical local and global evolution proofs. Lesson 10 constructs the Euclidean instanton, its smooth sphere bundle, exact characteristic charge and operator comparison. Lesson 11 develops states and probabilities, the harmonic oscillator and the free scalar field, including zero modes, operator domains and spectral sets. Lesson 12 constructs the compact-group projection, finite constraints and gauge slices, Grassmann determinants, BRST algebra and finite Ward identities. Lesson 13 derives the full one-loop coefficient and its regulator and scale comparisons. Lesson 14 constructs lattice fields, the finite measure, physical transfer, fixed-regulator spectrum and reflection form. Lesson 15 derives exact plaquette laws, controlled expansions, correct samplers and quantitative errors. Lesson 16 proves spectral measures, threshold extraction, static-source comparisons and explicit regulator limits. Lesson 17 constructs the continuum free field, its reflection quotient, Hamiltonian, observables and regulator maps. Lesson 18 connects established approaches and open problems through complete calculations and eight solved exercises.

The earlier standalone texts keep their own dependencies and original lesson numbers. Those numbers record their editions, not the new course order.
