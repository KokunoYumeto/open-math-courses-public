Six outward faces of a cube: exact U(1) holonomy and probability

This finite example keeps the eight vertices, twelve links, six outward faces and three physical lengths of one rectangular cube. It proves the complete edge-to-face map, its gauge kernel, its Haar pushforward, and the probability law obtained by putting the specified circle heat factor on each of its six faces. Every face time remains an independently specified positive number. A rectangular planar graph with all boundary links integrated is constructed separately and compared by explicit maps.

The cube boundary is itself a closed two-dimensional cellulated surface. Its relation is therefore already a closed-surface effect. The same six faces occur around a three-dimensional lattice cell; their relation shows why assigning independent variables to all faces of such cells does not repeat the free-boundary planar calculation. This example establishes no four-dimensional continuum measure or assertion about the Wilson-action continuum limit.

Complete companion to Unit 18. Proof labels CF.1–CF.18 and equations C1–C49 locate the complete arguments below.

CF.6. Exact Haar pushforward, including every gauge coordinate

as a change-of-variables identity for every bounded Borel integrand. Here and below such product identities mean equality of the integrals under the displayed bijections.
