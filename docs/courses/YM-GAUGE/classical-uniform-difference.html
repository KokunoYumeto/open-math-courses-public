Temporal differences, electric data and boundary increments

This Unit 9 chapter follows an actual difference through temporal heat smoothing, the electric field identity and physical-time reconstruction. Every estimate vanishes with its difference inputs. The final section calculates the remaining potential-wave coefficients and identifies the additional step required for a bound from the initial data alone.

Read the prescribed-interval heat comparison (FI), electric difference estimates (ED), temporal difference estimates (TD), potential differences (PD), wave estimates (HW), and spatial tension differences (TDI). The individual coefficients come from the finite wave bound (FC) and its linked heat estimates. The full physical and heat measures, speed, endpoint and gauge anchors are specified below.
