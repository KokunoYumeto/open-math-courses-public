The first moment of cubic Gauss sums

Patterson's first-moment conjecture for cubic Gauss sums, proved unconditionally after OpenAI's 2026 manuscript: the normalized cubic Gauss sums over the primary primes of Z[omega] of norm at most X add up to (6/5) c_* X^(5/6)/log X, with c_* = (2 pi)^(2/3)/(3 Gamma(2/3)). Cubic Gauss sums and their cubes; large sieves for cubic characters; the level Voronoi formula; exceptional character moments; corrected dispersion; an exact decomposition of the prime sum; the sharp cutoff; fixed angular modes and the powers G^k with k not divisible by 3.

Eight lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.
