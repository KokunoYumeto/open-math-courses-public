# Class field theory

This edition contains all twenty-four lessons of *Class field theory*. It begins with profinite Galois groups, cyclic cohomology, Hilbert 90 and radicals, proves abstract and local reciprocity, and constructs Lubin–Tate division fields. The local theory includes existence, local Kronecker–Weber, Hasse–Arf, conductors, Hilbert symbols, local conic criteria, Weil groups and rank-one factors in both characteristics.

The global theory proves the idèle-class Herbrand quotient, the norm-index bound, Hasse’s norm theorem, Artin reciprocity and existence, ray class fields, and Hilbert and ring class fields with explicit quadratic prime-form criteria. It includes Kronecker–Weber with the rational Artin map, Artin L-function identities and meromorphic functional equations, conductor integrality and the conductor–discriminant formula. Chebotarev is proved for Dirichlet density over all global fields, together with Bauer’s theorem and primes in ray classes. Power reciprocity includes rational and number-field primary quadratic laws and explicit cubic and quartic primary laws.

The final lesson proves the local Brauer invariant, the full Albert–Brauer–Hasse–Noether theorem, Tate’s fundamental-class isomorphism, the number-field Weil-group construction with topology and local compatibility, and the Golod–Shafarevich and unit-cohomology bounds for infinite class field towers. The function-field Weil construction is proved in lesson 17; the general Hasse–Minkowski proof is Theorem 22.7, after its ray-prime prerequisite, and uses the conic theorem of lesson 15. Earlier lessons link to these full proofs. The higher-dimensional local epsilon and orthogonal arguments are supplied in LG-GAL-06, Theorems 3.0 and 7.4; the lessons identify their precise role and distinguish them from the arguments proved here.
