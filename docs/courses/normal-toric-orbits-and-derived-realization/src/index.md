# Normal toric orbits and derived realization

A full proof of the orbit-stratification realization theorem for every complex normal separated toric variety of finite type, including singular and nonsimplicial cones. It constructs invariant affine neighborhoods and weighted cone charts, computes the injective maps of incident link groups, and proves unrestricted bounded-below realization over arbitrary unital rings and bounded finite-heart realization over every field. Three exercises have complete solutions.
