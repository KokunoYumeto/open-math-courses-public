Cell complexes, fundamental groups and covering spaces

Cell complexes, fundamental groups and covering spaces as used by the courses on group rings and classifying spaces: homotopy equivalences of cell complexes, trees, triangulations and simplicial approximation; fundamental groups of graphs and of complexes with attached cells, presentation complexes; universal covers with lifted cells and deck transformations, covers for normal subgroups, and cellular chains over the group ring with the Cayley complex.

Three lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  1. Cell complexes and homotopy equivalences
  2. Fundamental groups of graphs and two-complexes
  3. Universal covers and cellular chains

Theorems and assumptions · Reader and editable sources · Course record

  • The fundamental group and fundamental groupoid, the Seifert-van Kampen theorem, covering spaces and the universal cover, singular and cellular homology: core course Algebraic Topology, with the statements cited in the lessons.
  • Quotient topology, compactness and connectedness; groups, normal subgroups, group rings and free modules.
