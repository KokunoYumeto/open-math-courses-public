Taming implies compatibility

Donaldson asked whether, on a closed four-manifold, an almost complex structure tamed by a symplectic form admits a compatible symplectic form. The answer is yes. If not, separation gives a positive current that annihilates closed invariant forms, and a closed lift adds an anti-invariant correction so that the sum annihilates all closed forms. Resolvent regularization shows that the correction is square integrable and that nearby complex lines of the current align; at points of positive density the current is planar, and its restriction there is closed. A final pairing removes the correction, and the current then annihilates the taming form, which is impossible. Proved in the programme from one named hypothesis, the short-time asymptotics of the heat kernel of the Hodge Laplacian. The course is an exposition of OpenAI's proof (2026).

Five lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.
