A counterexample to Wall's D(2) problem

Wall's finite D(2) problem: is a finite complex of dimension three that looks two-dimensional to homology and cohomology with all coefficients homotopy equivalent to a finite complex of dimension two? Chain complexes twisted by a character of the fundamental group; Fox derivatives; an obstruction for complexes of dimension two proved by a tower of infinite cyclic covers; the finite plus construction; an explicit group that gives a counterexample. The new arguments are proved in full; of six standard theorems of algebraic topology, five are proved in the programme and the homotopy invariance of twisted homology is a named hypothesis. The course is an exposition of OpenAI's construction (2026).

Four lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  1. Wall's D(2) problem and twisted homology
  2. The tower obstruction
  3. The finite plus construction
  4. The counterexample
