Additive indecomposability of the primes

No finite modification of the set of primes is a sum A + B of two sets with at least two elements each: Ostmann's conjecture, the inverse Goldbach problem, after OpenAI's 2026 paper. Sieve bounds and collision stability; decorrelation from translated quadratic and higher characters (Bonami's inequality, Weyl sums, Heath-Brown's quadratic large sieve, Kneser's theorem, anchor codes); a supply of nonsparse Fourier transforms from coverage of every large prime; a finite-field tree comparison; a positive statistic and its contradiction.

18 lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.
