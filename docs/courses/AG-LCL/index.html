Local cohomology, Lefschetz theorems and purity

Local cohomology and duality, connectedness, formal geometry, Lefschetz comparison, Picard groups, purity and specialization. All eight lessons include proofs, examples and solved exercises.

  3. Cohomological dimension, vanishing and connectedness

  5. Lefschetz theorems for finite étale covers
  6. Picard groups and Grothendieck–Lefschetz
  7. Purity of the branch locus
  8. Specialization and tame ramification
