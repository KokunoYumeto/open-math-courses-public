Hypercontractivity on a torus and juntas for Lipschitz functions

Trigonometric polynomials and Fejer means on a torus; the heat operators and three inequalities for them. A logarithmic Sobolev inequality on the circle, with Rothaus's lemma. Hypercontractivity of the heat operators on the circle and on a torus of any dimension. Austin's theorem: a function on a cube that is Lipschitz for the sup distance is close to a function of a bounded number of coordinates, with an explicit bound that does not depend on the dimension. All proofs are complete; no theorem of the literature is assumed.

Four lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  1. Trigonometric polynomials and the heat operators on a torus
  2. A logarithmic Sobolev inequality on the circle
  3. Hypercontractivity of the heat operators
  4. Lipschitz functions are close to juntas
