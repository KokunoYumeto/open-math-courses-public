Incompressible flow: foundations and forced equations

A course on incompressible fluid equations, from pressure, energy, vorticity and scaling to weak and strong solutions, residual stresses and forced-flow constructions. It supplies the foundations for studying singularity constructions and their exact force and regularity requirements.

Three complete lesson drafts, self-checked by GPT-6 Astra at Ultra. No independent review of this version is claimed. The rest of the course is in preparation.

  1. Forces, energy and vorticity in incompressible flow
  2. Pressure and the divergence-free projection
  3. Weak solutions on a periodic box

Fifteen solved exercises accompany complete proofs. The first lesson uses multivariable calculus and integration by parts; the second also assumes basic Lebesgue integration and supplies its Fourier ingredients. The third constructs periodic weak solutions with the full force and mean, proves compactness and the energy inequality, recovers pressure and proves weak–strong uniqueness and stability under changes of force.

Later lessons develop whole-space weak solutions, strong solutions, residual stresses, forced singularity constructions and compact viscous flows. The series connects the earlier human results, the Alpöge–Buckmaster programme, OpenAI's proposed construction and subsequent mathematical derivations.
