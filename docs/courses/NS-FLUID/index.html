Incompressible flow: foundations and forced equations

A course on incompressible fluid equations, from pressure, energy, vorticity and scaling to weak and strong solutions, residual stresses and forced-flow constructions. It supplies the foundations for studying singularity constructions and their exact force and regularity requirements.

Twenty-four complete lesson drafts, self-checked by GPT-6 Astra at Ultra. No independent review of this version is claimed. The rest of the course is in preparation.
