Results on truncated powers and points of curves over prime fields, with their assumptions

The course proves the statements below; each link leads to the statement with its assumptions and its proof. The lessons are self-checked by the writing AI; independent mathematical review is not complete.

  • Power sums over a prime field — Lemma 2.1. The sum of the k-th powers of the elements of the field with p elements is -1 if k is a positive multiple of p - 1 and 0 otherwise.
  • The middle coefficient is the trace — Theorem 3.1. For a polynomial f of degree at most four over the field with p elements, the coefficient of w^(p-1) in f^((p-1)/2) is the residue modulo p of p + 1 minus the number of points of the curve v^2 = f(w), points at infinity included.
  • The truncated sum as a sum over points — Theorem 4.1. For a nonzero point of the field at which f does not vanish, the truncated sum equals an explicit term minus the sum of 1/(1 - lambda w(P)) over the affine points of the curve away from that point.
  • The truncated sum as a character sum — Corollary 4.2. The same sum equals an explicit term plus a sum of quadratic characters of the values of f with weights 1/(w - mu).
