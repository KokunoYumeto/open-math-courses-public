A three-manifold without conjugate points

A closed three-manifold with a Riemannian metric without conjugate points that admits no metric of nonpositive sectional curvature. Two holed-torus-times-circle pieces are glued so that the boundary classes satisfy h_2 = h_1 + f_1 and f_2 = h_1; shifts of axes and a Pythagorean length formula in spaces with the CN inequality show that no nonpositively curved metric exists, and a concave cometric on the neck, with an index-form analysis of every visit of a geodesic to the neck, excludes conjugate points. The course is an exposition of OpenAI's proof (2026).

Seven lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.

  1. Conjugate points and index forms
  2. The manifold and its fundamental group
  3. Spaces with the CN inequality
  4. Axes, shifts and the two-piece obstruction
  5. A concave cometric and convex collars
  6. Neck passages and the index form
  7. Returning passages and the main theorem
