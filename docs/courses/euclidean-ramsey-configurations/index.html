The classification of finite Euclidean Ramsey sets

OpenAI's classification (2026) of the finite Euclidean Ramsey sets: a finite set is Ramsey exactly when a linear system over the tensor square of its coordinate field has a solution. The colouring obstruction from rational functionals, a finite lattice identity through the flatness of completion, two-scale configurations by Gaussian smoothing, the Hales–Jewett theorem, ultrafilter semigroups and a path criterion in a free group, and the correction of endpoints through derived series. Examples on circles: at most five concyclic points are Ramsey, including kites that embed in no finite transitive set; nine generic points and three rotated squares are not.

Nine lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.
