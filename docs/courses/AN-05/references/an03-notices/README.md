# AN-03 prerequisite readings

These three independently authored programme chapters provide the named Fourier, finite-dimensional, local elliptic and smooth-coordinate readings used by Subellipticity and unique continuation. They retain their complete mathematical text, citations and declared assumptions.

The reading sources are Markdown with LaTeX mathematics. The text is dedicated to CC0 1.0; see [rights and licenses](RIGHTS.md), [authorship](TITLE_PAGE.md) and [history](HISTORY.md). The historical FSF license document is preserved separately and does not license these chapters.

Links to other parent-course chapters or illustrations identify further readings; those files are included only where explicitly supplied. Inclusion of these three chapters does not admit the entire AN-03 course or broaden its recorded review scopes.
