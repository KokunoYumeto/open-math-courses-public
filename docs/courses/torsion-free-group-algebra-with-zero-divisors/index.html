A torsion-free group algebra with zero divisors

There is a finitely presented torsion-free group G, with a finite two-dimensional classifying space, whose group algebra over the field with two elements has zero divisors: Kaplansky's zero-divisor conjecture fails. Two random immersed labelled graphs with prescribed letter sets of odd intersections, built from the projective plane over the field with 128 elements, make every contribution to a product cancel in pairs; a word-counting estimate, planar separation and cone pictures show that the group of the graphs is torsion-free and that the factors are nonzero. The course is an exposition of OpenAI's proof (2026).

Ten lessons with proofs, examples and exercises with solutions. Written and self-checked by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic); independent review is not complete.
