Open Mathematics CoursesCourse contents (Cotype, K-convexity and the approximation property)

Results on Walsh norms, moment lifts, sign matrices and compact factorizations

The course proves the statements below; each link leads to the statement with its assumptions and its proof. The lessons are self-checked by the writing AI; independent mathematical review is not complete.

  • The cotype-cotype characterization — Theorem 2.1. Assume (P) and (MP) (Pisier; not proved in the programme). A nonzero real Banach space with the approximation property is K-convex if and only if it and its dual have finite cotype.
  • The decay theorem — Theorem 2.3. Under the approximation property and finite cotype of the space and its dual, the Walsh norms of the identity tend to 0 and the space contains no uniform copies of l_1^n; proved in full in the course.
  • Duality of K-convexity — Lemma 3.1. K(X*) = K(X): the Rademacher projection on L_2(X*) is the adjoint of the one on L_2(X).
  • K-convex spaces contain no uniform l_1^n — Proposition 3.3. K(l_1^(2^n)) >= sqrt(n/3), so a K-convex space does not contain the spaces l_1^n uniformly.
  • Walsh norms — Lemma 1.1. Walsh norms lie between 2^(-n/2)|T| and |T|, are nonincreasing, invariant under amplification, and decay like 2^(-n/2) for each finite-rank operator.
  • Kahane's inequality — Lemma 2.1. For Rademacher sums in a Banach space, the L_2 norm is at most 24 times the L_1 norm.
  • Moment lifts — Lemma 3.2. Under fixed-length cotype bounds, bounded sign-indexed families with prescribed first moments exist.
  • Chebyshev polynomials — Lemma 1.1. Coefficients of a polynomial of degree d in the Chebyshev basis are at most twice its sup norm on [-1,1], and |Q'(0)| <= d(d+1) times the sup norm.
  • A coefficient of binary rank two — Lemma 2.1. An odd bounded function of a sign matrix whose row and column averages reproduce odd functions with large first-order coefficients has a Fourier coefficient of binary rank at least two, bounded below.
  • Pairs of frames — Lemma 1.1. GL(n, F_2) acts transitively on pairs of frames with a prescribed pairing matrix.
  • Growth from a slow decrease — Lemma 2.1. If w_(n-2m)(T) <= 2 w_n(T), then w_(2(n-2m))(T) >= c_1 w_n(T), with c_1 depending only on the cotype data.
  • Uniform decay — Theorem 1.1. Walsh norms of finite-rank operators and their norm limits decay to 0 at a rate depending only on fixed-length cotype bounds.
  • A compact domain — Lemma 2.1. Every finite-dimensional subspace of X factors through an injective compact map J: G -> X with |J| <= 2 and c_m(G*) <= 12 c_m(X*).
  • Grothendieck pairs — Corollary 4.1. Assume (P), (GMR), (FTJ). If (E, F) is a Grothendieck pair with F infinite-dimensional with the approximation property, then (E, l_2) is a Grothendieck pair.

Reading order

Five lessons, to be read in order. The decay theorem is proved in full. The equivalence with K-convexity uses the named hypotheses (P) and (MP), two theorems of Pisier; the corollary on Grothendieck pairs also uses (GMR) and (FTJ). None of these is proved in the programme.
