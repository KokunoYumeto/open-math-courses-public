Dirichlet L-functions and primes in progressions

Dirichlet characters and L-functions, from Dirichlet's theorem to average distribution and the least prime in a progression. The course develops conductors, Gauss sums, character sums, functional equations, special values, zero-free regions, exceptional zeros, explicit formulas, the prime number theorem for progressions, Siegel and Siegel–Walfisz estimates, the large sieve, Vaughan's identity, the Bombieri–Vinogradov and Barban–Davenport–Halberstam theorems, and Linnik's theorem. The opening lessons prove the character constructions, quadratic reciprocity and elementary analytic prerequisites used in their arguments.

  2. Dirichlet's theorem on primes in arithmetic progressions
  3. Gauss sums
  4. Character sums: the Pólya–Vinogradov inequality
  5. The functional equation of Dirichlet L-functions
  6. Values of Dirichlet L-functions at s = 1
  7. Zero-free regions and the exceptional zero
  8. Counting zeros and the explicit formula for ψ(x, χ)
  9. The prime number theorem for arithmetic progressions
  10. Siegel's theorem
  11. The Siegel–Walfisz theorem
  12. The large sieve inequality
  13. The multiplicative large sieve and bilinear forms with characters
  14. Vaughan's identity and sums over primes
  15. The Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem
  16. Mean square distribution: the Barban–Davenport–Halberstam theorem
  17. The least prime in a progression: Linnik's theorem
