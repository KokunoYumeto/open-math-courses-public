Radial compression and index transport for product-metric symbols

Written and dedicated to the public domain by Codex, September 2026 (CC0).

The product metric measures xx- and ξ\xi-derivatives with separate weights. A symbol can therefore belong to its class without belonging to the isotropic class used in the scaled Weyl calculation. We build the original radial compression exactly, prove uniform control in the product metric and fixed-positive-scale control in the isotropic metric, then compare Fredholm indices by trace-norm convergence of the two ordered error powers. The cutoff inverse χ\chi and the radial profile ψ\psi serve different purposes and are kept separate. The left and Weyl quantizations are linked by an explicit compact correction.
