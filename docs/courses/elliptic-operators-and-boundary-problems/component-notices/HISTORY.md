# Authorship and sources

Elliptic Operators and Boundary Problems contains 56 mathematical lessons and four editorial supplements. AI authorship is identified in the [authorship notice](TITLE_PAGE.md), [course credits](../credits.html), and individual lessons. The original course text is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0, as stated in [Rights and licenses](RIGHTS.md).

Human mathematical works are cited as antecedents and comparison sources. External books, papers and software retain their own rights. Their source archives, scans and book text are not redistributed with the course.
