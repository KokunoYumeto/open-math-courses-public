Faithful-state expectations from full bounded-graph modular data

Let MMM have a faithful normal state φ\varphiφ, and let B⊂MB\subset MB⊂M be a von Neumann subalgebra with the same identity. If BBB is invariant under the modular group of φ\varphiφ, we construct its normal φ\varphiφ-preserving expectation by compressing the faithful-state GNS representation. The central step identifies the entire smaller Tomita graph. Gaussian filters make that identification without assuming a general weight expectation or a KMS uniqueness theorem.

The proof applies to arbitrary Hilbert spaces and nonseparable algebras. It concerns faithful normal states; no assertion about infinite values of a general weight is used. If M=0M=0M=0, the unique map and both GNS spaces are zero and all conclusions have their zero-space meanings. Below M≠0M\ne0M=0.

The exact inputs are BG1–BG6 for the full left-Hilbert-algebra modular graphs, commutant theorem and modular normalization, normal GNS representations, bounded/unbounded spectral calculus, the scalar Gaussian Fourier identity, normal vector pairings and the complete predual, and the increasing-net normality criterion. The general modular input BG is constructed from the freely accessible bounded-operator proof of Rieffel and Van Daele, cited below.

Faithful star representations reflect positivity, including at every matrix size.
