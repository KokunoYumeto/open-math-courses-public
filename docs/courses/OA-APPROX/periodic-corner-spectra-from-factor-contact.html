Set ω=2π/P\omega=2\pi/Pω=2π/P and N=MαN=M^\alphaN=Mα. Inner products follow the second-variable-linear convention of this course. The concrete predual and its dual are proved in H03. The classical definitions are Connes's action spectrum and fixed-corner intersection. We prove their periodic specialization directly. The proof does not require a spectrum theorem for arbitrary locally compact groups, a spectral-synthesis theorem, or a general dual-center kernel theorem.

PP03. Contact in the fixed factor

The fixed algebra NNN is a von Neumann algebra: it is the intersection of the ultraweakly closed kernels of x↦αt(x)−xx\mapsto\alpha_t(x)-xx↦αt​(x)−x, and it is closed under products and adjoints. The support and join constructions cited above therefore apply inside NNN.

For a projection p∈Np\in Np∈N, form

zN(p)=⋁u∈U(N)upu∗.(PP11) z_N(p)=\bigvee_{u\in\mathcal U(N)}upu^*. \tag{PP11}zN​(p)=u∈U(N)⋁​upu∗.(PP11)

Conjugation by any unitary permutes the family, so it fixes the join. Every element of NNN is a linear combination of its unitaries: for a self-adjoint contraction aaa, the positive root c=(1−a2)1/2c=(1-a^2)^{1/2}c=(1−a2)1/2 commutes with aaa, a+ica+ica+ic is unitary, and a=((a+ic)+(a−ic))/2a=((a+ic)+(a-ic))/2a=((a+ic)+(a−ic))/2. Scale and split real and imaginary parts for the general element. Thus the join in (PP11) is central. It majorizes ppp, and every central projection majorizing ppp majorizes all its conjugates and their join. In a factor it is one whenever p≠0p\ne0p=0.

If two nonzero projections p,q∈Np,q\in Np,q∈N had pNq=0pNq=0pNq=0, then for all unitaries u,v∈Nu,v\in Nu,v∈N,

(upu∗)(vqv∗)=u p(u∗v)q v∗=0.(PP12) (upu^*)(vqv^*)=u\,p(u^*v)q\,v^*=0. \tag{PP12}(upu∗)(vqv∗)=up(u∗v)qv∗=0.(PP12)

The ranges of every conjugate of ppp and every conjugate of qqq would be orthogonal. Their closed spans, which are the ranges of the two joins (PP11), would also be orthogonal. Both joins are one, a contradiction. Hence

p,q∈Proj⁡(N)∖{0}⟹pNq≠0.(PP13) p,q\in\operatorname{Proj}(N)\setminus\{0\} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad pNq\ne0. \tag{PP13}p,q∈Proj(N)∖{0}⟹pNq=0.(PP13)

This proof uses no projection-dimension or trace comparison theorem.

The countable faithful CAR product has separable predual at this application. Its Hilbert space is the completion of the increasing finite tensor spaces, each finite-dimensional.
