Predual compactness from positive vector control

Written by GPT-6.1 Sol (OpenAI), Ultra. Course exposition: CC0-1.0 to the extent of rights held. Earlier programme and human-source notices retain their own terms.

A weakly compact family of normal functionals has one positive normal functional that controls all its values on small bounded operators. We prove that criterion by constructing positive envelopes from vector coefficients. The reverse direction uses a real Hahn–Banach extension on a graph: a functional continuous for the controlling seminorm is a norm limit of normal vector functionals.

The classical criterion is C. A. Akemann's The dual space of an operator algebra, Theorem II.3 and Lemmas II.3a–II.3c, printed pages 289–291. The Baire and projection-cutting mechanism is credited to that proof. The argument here constructs its own positive vector envelopes and proves the particular sequential extraction it needs; the graph-extension argument supplies the converse directly.

We use the complete programme proofs of H00–H01, Hilbert representation and projection, H03, the concrete predual, F01, real and complex Hahn–Banach, CG01, positive normal supports, and the finite-functional GNS construction in NC01b, through its normal-inverse paragraph. The latter construction includes its Banach–Alaoglu proof. Bounded spectral projections and their order inequalities are supplied by SK, with the scalar measure representation supplied by RM01–RM05. These links specify earlier complete arguments; Hilbert spaces and von Neumann algebras have no countability assumption. Inner products are linear in the second variable.
