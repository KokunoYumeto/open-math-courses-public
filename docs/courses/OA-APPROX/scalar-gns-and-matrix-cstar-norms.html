Scalar GNS and matrix C∗-norms

Let AAA be any complex C∗-algebra, including the zero algebra. No separability or nondegeneracy is assumed. We first construct representations from scalar order, then construct the matrix norms, and finally prove complete positivity and complete contractivity. Inner products here are linear in the second variable.

Our scalar inputs are choice and Hahn–Banach, continuous calculus, positivity and order, Hilbert space facts, forced unitization, and inner product completion. These are scalar proofs. They do not use a matrix C∗-norm or a dilation of a completely positive map. Their set-theoretic boundary is ordinary choice, and their analytic boundary is the real and complex scalar fields, completeness and elementary real calculus stated there.

The arrows record the order of the proofs below. The first three steps use scalar positivity only. The matrix square root is taken after the matrix algebra has been proved complete with a C∗-norm. This order applies to arbitrary AAA; the extra scalar summand in its forced unitization also handles A=0A=0A=0.
