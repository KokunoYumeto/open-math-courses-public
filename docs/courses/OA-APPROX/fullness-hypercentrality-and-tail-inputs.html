Finite corners, unitary displacement and the tensor tail

Programme exposition: CC0-1.0 to the extent of rights held. Human-source terms remain separate.

The fullness and hypercentrality lesson needs three constructions beyond the centralizing quotient: division of a finite algebra with its center retained, a unitary moving a trace-zero element, and an exact map onto a tensor tail. Its nonfactor example also needs a proof of its center and type, rather than an appeal to unspecified fibers.

We use the complete retained CF calculus and Hilbert arguments, SC00–07 measure and Lp proofs, CP preduals, BD bounded density, PC0–7/FCT0–7, NT1–2, FG2's complete dyadic factor model, and CQ2's trace representation. Inner products below are linear in the second variable; conjugating SC07's convention gives this convention with the same Hilbert norm.
