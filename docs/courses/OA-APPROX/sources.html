Sources, authorship and component terms

This graduate course develops completely positive maps, matrix approximation, nuclearity, infinite tensor products, central sequences, MASAs and the injectivity/AFD equivalence. It contains fifty lessons, 327 numbered results and 504 exercises with complete solutions.

The reading list credits the research articles and notes used in the exposition. The result register identifies the associated arguments.
