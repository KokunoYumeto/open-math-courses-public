Positive functionals and essential cyclic vectors

Let AAA be any complex C∗-algebra. A positive functional takes nonnegative real values on positive elements. Its boundedness, even when continuity is not initially assumed, is proved in MG05. We write r=∥f∥r=\|f\|r=∥f∥. Inner products are linear in the second variable.

The scalar calculus, order, Hilbert representation and forced unitization are proved in CF1–10. MG01–MG03 gives scalar positive extensions, the unital GNS construction and arbitrary-cardinality faithful representations. F09 constructs a positive contractive two-sided approximate identity (ei)(e_i)(ei​), with no monotonicity or countability requirement. ODF02 proves its strong convergence to the projection onto a representation's essential space.

We prove the norm limit needed for the nonunital cyclic vector, then the extension and essential-space assertions. The zero functional and zero algebra are included. For a nonzero algebra, the resulting states also norm self-adjoint elements and detect their order. These are the scalar inputs to the universal state representation and bidual construction.
