How much can two commutators reveal about an operator? In a free-group factor they bound its entire distance from the scalars. In the dyadic tensor factor RRR, a unitary can instead escape into a later tensor position, commute asymptotically with every fixed operator, and stay a definite distance from every scalar.

We compute these examples first. We then relate their behavior to the topology of automorphism groups, retaining the whole center for algebras that are not factors. The original result and problem numbers are stable labels for those connections.

The problems are interleaved with the arguments they test. Every problem has its complete solution immediately below it. The foundations prove the normal-functional, faithful-state, metric, Borel-image and phase constructions, and the two factor constructions used here.
