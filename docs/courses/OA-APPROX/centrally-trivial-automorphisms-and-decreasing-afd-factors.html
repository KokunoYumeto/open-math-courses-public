Uniform displacement and finite normal-functional tests answer different questions. The first detects an outer automorphism by looking arbitrarily far down a tensor tail. The second lets an approximately inner automorphism be compared with a centrally trivial one. We first construct the tail and fixed-algebra examples, then prove the two-state comparison and finish the abstract group criterion.

The complete CT0–5 inputs supply normal transport, approximate innerness of every automorphism of RRR, the tensor relative commutant, finite and infinite Bernoulli products, direct irreducibility of the finite fixed algebras, and compact twisted-orbit minimization. The strong-stability lesson supplies the whole absorption theorem; the quotient and exact-lift lesson and fullness/corner lesson supply the ordinary and all-ultrafilter alternatives at full factor scope.
