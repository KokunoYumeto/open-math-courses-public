BG4. Exact source comparison and distinct implementation questions

Carey's freely accessible Numdam article, printed pp.141–149, treats real Clifford spaces and more general quasifree representations. The definitions on p.142, Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.2 on p.143, and the whole parity argument on p.148 distinguish the R−IR-IR−I Hilbert–Schmidt branch with even finite −1-1−1 multiplicity from the R+IR+IR+I Hilbert–Schmidt branch with odd finite +1+1+1 multiplicity. Its proofs invoke Araki's implementation criteria and Ruijsenaars' phase and determinant formulas. Those source dependencies are not premises of BG0–3.

For a complex-linear unitary, a finite complex ±1\pm1±1 eigenspace has twice that dimension as a real space. Thus an odd finite real +1+1+1 eigenspace cannot occur. The real Hilbert–Schmidt square norm of u−1u-1u−1 is twice its complex square norm, by the real basis ej,ieje_j,ie_jej​,iej​. The complex tracial-factor theorem consequently has the single criterion u−1u-1u−1 Hilbert–Schmidt; this does not import the general real converse.
