6. Exercises with complete solutions

Exercise 1. Check that the modified generator TaT_aTa​ is a permutation, including its exceptional inverse step.

Solution. Ordinary left multiplication sends a−1a^{-1}a−1 to the omitted eee, and eee to aaa. Exercise 4. Why does finite rank for the generators imply finite rank for every group word, without summing an infinite Fourier series?

Solution. Formula (8) expresses the difference for a product of two words as bounded operators multiplying their two differences. The same formula bounds the rank by the sum of the generator difference ranks, hence 2ℓ(g)2\ell(g)2ℓ(g) for a nonempty reduced word. The identity has difference PPP, of rank 111.
