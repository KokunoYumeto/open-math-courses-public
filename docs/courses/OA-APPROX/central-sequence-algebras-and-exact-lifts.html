Central sequence algebras and exact lifts

Written by GPT-6.1 Sol (OpenAI), Ultra. New original exposition: CC0-1.0 to the extent of rights held. Comparison sources retain their own terms.

Matrix units require exact equations: two diagonal projections must be orthogonal, and a connecting partial isometry must have the prescribed initial and final projections. A limit can make those equations exact while every coordinate still has an error. This lesson explains how to repair the coordinates without changing their limit.

We first examine the two repairs that will be needed: moving finite trace mass, and making room at infinite endpoints. We then construct the limiting algebra, lift one projection, prove completeness of its trace metric, and assemble the repaired matrix units. Original result and problem numbers remain stable labels throughout this order.

Our setting is a nonzero factor MMM with separable predual, a faithful normal state φ\varphiφ, and a free ultrafilter ω\omegaω on N\mathbb NN. The complete quotient and lifting inputs prove CQ1–7. The preceding central-sequence lesson proves the passage from predual commutators to bounded strong* centrality; NT1–2 proves the normal-functional and faithful-state topology facts used below.

The quotient norm, faithful trace representation, whole-domain normality, finite cancellation, small infinite pieces, minimal-projection test and partition error are proved in CQ1–7. Their complete earlier inputs are the retained CF, CP, SC, BD, PC, FCT, NT and AF arguments, with their existing notices. This supplies the construction before any appeal to an external ultraproduct theorem.

Alain Connes, Outer conjugacy classes of automorphisms of factors, Propositions 1.1.1–1.1.3 and Lemmas 1.1.4–1.1.5, printed pp.387–390 (PDF pp.6–9), is the historical free comparison for the scalar trace and exact repairs. Proposition 1.1.1 recalls an earlier result rather than proving completeness there. Proposition 1.1.3(b) explicitly requires equivalent coordinate endpoints. Its proof removes small pieces at both infinite endpoints; our repair instead removes a piece and its image from the polar partial isometry, controlling both state masses. We use a fixed cutoff 1/21/21/2, prove finite-subpiece cancellation through a finite join, and retain every nonunital matrix support.

Adrian Ocneanu, Actions of discrete amenable groups on factors, Chapter 5, Section 5.1, printed pp.47–50 (PDF pp.61–64), proves completeness and normality for the normalizer ultraproduct and its centralizing subalgebra. The objects there are distinguished: the normalizer quotient is larger. Our proof constructs only the centralizing quotient, using its tracial metric and predual tests, and obtains normal automorphisms by their trace Hilbert unitaries.

Hiroshi Ando and Uffe Haagerup, Ultraproducts of von Neumann algebras, v3, Section 3.1 and Propositions 3.14–3.15, gives the complementary normalizer/corner construction.
