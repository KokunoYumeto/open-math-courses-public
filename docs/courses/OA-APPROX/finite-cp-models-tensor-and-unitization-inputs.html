Complete inputs for completely positive finite models

Finite matrix models have to work before a unit is available, on Hilbert spaces of arbitrary cardinality, and for operator systems that need not be closed. This component supplies those inputs for Completely positive finite models. It also proves the representation of an arbitrary commutative C*-algebra needed for the lesson's unnumbered nuclearity assertion.

The scalar boundary is the complete CF1–10 constructions: choice, Hahn–Banach, compact products, continuous C*-calculus, order, forced unitization and Hilbert/Banach completion. The complete MG01–06 arguments then construct scalar GNS representations, faithful representations, canonical matrix C*-norms, positive scalar functionals and complete contractivity of star homomorphisms. Matrix square roots are used only after that matrix construction. CP1–6 supplies the whole concrete coefficient predual. Inner products in the dilation arguments below are linear in their second variable.
