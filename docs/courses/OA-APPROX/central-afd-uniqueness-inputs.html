AF1. Finite matrices and their trace projections

Let DDD be any finite-dimensional unital star subalgebra of a von Neumann algebra. It is a C-star algebra: finite-dimensional subspaces are norm closed, as follows by minimizing the norm on the Euclidean unit sphere of a chosen basis; its multiplication and involution are inherited. For completeness, this minimization uses compactness of that finite sphere and injectivity of the basis map. It gives a strictly positive minimum, proving equivalence of its coordinate and operator norms.
