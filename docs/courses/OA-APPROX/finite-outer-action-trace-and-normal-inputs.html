The finite outer-action lesson needs the whole tracial commutant, a faithful normal range with a normal inverse, the central kernel of a normal homomorphism, and the actual matrix-base Bernoulli products. These arguments cover arbitrary Hilbert spaces and finite factors without a separability assumption.

The complete earlier inputs are CF's calculus, Hahn–Banach and Hilbert constructions, CP's concrete preduals, and BD's bounded density, NT1–2, PC/FCT, FH0's normal canonical finite-factor trace, AF1–5, and SS0–2's finite matrix coordinates and whole tracial products. Inner products are linear in the second variable.
