Normal automorphism transport and Bernoulli fixed algebras

Programme exposition: CC0-1.0 to the extent of rights held. Human-source terms remain separate.

The centrally trivial automorphism lesson uses general factors with separable predual, an arbitrary finite-factor displacement argument, and two different Bernoulli index groups. The finite group supplies noncommuting outer classes. The infinite group supplies decreasing fixed algebras with scalar intersection. We prove the needed constructions at those full scopes.

The complete earlier inputs are CF, CP and BD, NT1–2 and the dyadic model FG2, PC/FCT comparison and the normal finite trace, AF1–5, FH0's normality and uniqueness of finite-factor tracial states, and SS0–3's exact coordinates, whole variable product and spatial splitting. Inner products are linear in the second variable.
