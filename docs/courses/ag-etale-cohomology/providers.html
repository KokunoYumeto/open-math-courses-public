Algebra and Galois reading routes

Module prerequisites for Wedderburn–Artin

Schur’s lemma and complete reducibility for arbitrary unital modules, with finite-length arguments where explicitly required.

Schur’s lemma · Complete reducibility · Wedderburn–Artin and uniqueness

Regular trace and reduced trace

The regular-trace criterion is stated in characteristic zero. The positive-characteristic matrix example explains why reduced trace is a different invariant; it is not a characteristic-zero restriction on Brauer groups.

Regular trace and its positive-characteristic limitation · Reduced trace and reduced norm

Continuous Hilbert 90

Further reading for the profinite continuation: every field, continuous cocycles, and discrete separable-closure coefficients. The finite-extension descent results do not require this continuation as an extra hypothesis.

Finite quotient cochains, Proposition 3.1 · Hilbert 90, Theorem 4.1 · Editable source

Commutative-algebra reading routes

Prime separation, radicals, localization and minimal primes hold without a Noetherian hypothesis. Finite irreducible decomposition is stated with its Noetherian-topology condition. Polynomial dimension and normalization here concern algebras over a field.

Hilbert–Samuel growth and local dimension

For finite modules over Noetherian local rings and ideals of definition, the associated graded construction gives eventual polynomial growth. Artin-Rees gives top-coefficient additivity, not additivity of whole polynomials. Local dimension is identified in its separate theorem before use for completion.

  • Hilbert-Serre with the multiplication kernel
  • Hilbert-Samuel existence and generator bound, Theorem 4.2
  • Degree and top-coefficient exact-sequence formula, Theorem 6.1
  • Artin-Rees, Theorem 5.1
  • Nakayama, Theorem 4.2
  • Local dimension, Theorem 2.1
  • Completion reading

Finite prime avoidance and module dimension

Finite prime avoidance supplies parameters over every residue field. A finite module over a Noetherian ring admits a finite prime filtration; filtration primes need not all be associated to the whole module. These are the stated inputs of local ring and module dimension.

  • Finite prime avoidance, Solution 8.5
  • Prime filtration and associated-prime finiteness, Theorem 2.2
  • Associated-prime existence and zero divisors, Theorem 1.2
  • Parameter-count and module-dimension consumers
  • Minimal primes and finite irreducible decomposition

Affine modules and pullback

For any ring, modules and quasi-coherent sheaves on its spectrum are equivalent. Along an arbitrary morphism, pullback on compatible affine opens is extension of scalars. No Noetherian or finite-generation hypothesis is imposed; the separate pushforward statement has its own hypotheses.

  • Affine equivalence, Theorem 3.2
  • Pullback, Theorem 3.1
  • Quasi-coherent cohomology consumer, Proposition 4.2

Finite extensions of the complex Laurent-series field

Finite separable normalization over a Noetherian normal domain, simple-root lifting in complete local rings, local factors of a finite algebra over a henselian ring, dimension under integral inclusion, the one-dimensional normal local DVR criterion, and completeness of finite modules. These apply to the displayed integral closure over C[[t]].
