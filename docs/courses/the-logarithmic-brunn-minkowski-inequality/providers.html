Results on Wulff bodies and the reduction of the logarithmic Brunn-Minkowski inequality

The course proves the statements below; each link leads to the statement with its assumptions and its proof. The lessons are self-checked by the writing AI; independent mathematical review is not complete.

  • Support functions of convex bodies — Lemma 1.1. The support function of a convex body is Lipschitz; the body is the set of the x with <x,u> <= h_K(u) for all unit vectors u; for K = -K it is even and bounded below by a positive number; it is additive for Minkowski combinations.
  • Wulff bodies — Lemma 1.2. The Wulff body W[f] of a positive continuous function on the sphere is a convex body between the balls of radius min f and max f; it is monotone and homogeneous in f; W[h_K] = K; W[h_K^(1-l) h_L^l] lies in (1-l)K + lL for symmetric K, L.
  • The logarithmic Brunn-Minkowski inequality follows from the variance bound — Theorem 2.2. If Var(sum b_i psi_i) <= sum b_i^2 E psi_i holds for all densities proportional to exp(-eps|x|^2/2 - sum psi_i), psi_i = (<p_i,x>/h_i)^q, in dimension n >= 2, then |W[h_K^(1-l) h_L^l]| >= |K|^(1-l) |L|^l for all origin-symmetric convex bodies in R^n; in dimension 1 equality holds.
  • Second derivative of the logarithm of the integral of a soft polytope — Lemma 3.1. For widths h_i(t) = (h_i^0)^(1-t) (h_i^1)^t: (log Z)'' = Var(sum b_i psi_i) - sum b_i^2 E psi_i with b_i = -q log(h_i^1/h_i^0).
  • The additive inequality for 0 < p < 1 from the logarithmic one — Corollary 6.1. If the logarithmic Brunn-Minkowski inequality holds in dimension n, then |W[((1-l) h_K^p + l h_L^p)^(1/p)]|^(p/n) >= (1-l)|K|^(p/n) + l|L|^(p/n) for origin-symmetric convex bodies and 0 < p < 1.
