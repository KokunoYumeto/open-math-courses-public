Mixed symbols on every real two-parameter Sobolev scale

This lesson proves continuity on the full two-parameter Sobolev scale. The orders are arbitrary real numbers, the base variable ranges over all of ℝn\mathbb R^n, and the symbol can be a rectangular matrix between fixed finite-dimensional Hermitian spaces. The original weights 1+|ξ|1+|\xi| and 1+|ξ′|1+|\xi'| remain explicit.
