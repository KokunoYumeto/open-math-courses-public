Source and edition notice: holomorphic functions and power series

This programme reading proves the polydisc Cauchy formula, convergent Taylor expansion, coefficient bounds, smoothness, and the converse from a locally convergent power series. Definition 1.1, Theorems 1.2 and 2.1, and Proposition 2.2 use the preceding programme readings on closed-interval real analysis, the complex exponential and circle, and the scalar Cauchy formula.
