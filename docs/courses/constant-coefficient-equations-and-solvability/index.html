Constant-coefficient equations and solvability

Polynomial symbols connect support geometry with regularity and solvability. The topics include fundamental solutions, operator strength, propagation, hyperbolic equations, Cauchy and boundary problems, polynomial division, global solvability and convolution systems.

A draft course with 214 lessons, worked examples, exercises and solutions. Individual lessons name their prerequisite assumptions, including supporting results whose proofs are still planned.
