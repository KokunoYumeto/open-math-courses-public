# Rational compact cohomology and the normal-circle connecting map

This original proof constructs the actual rational-to-real compact-cohomology coefficient map, proves its compatibility with the chosen orientation class and unshifted right cap, and proves that it becomes an isomorphism after scalar extension. It verifies the coefficient squares for closed restriction, open excision extension and the global finite-chain Thom tube. The CO and FC real comparison then fixes a rational connecting map with coefficient (−1)^p in the normal-circle-first convention; faithful field extension proves its closed–open sequence is exact.

Read `rational-compact-coefficient-bridge.md`. It contains the complete argument, three worked examples, six exercises with complete solutions and two original figures. The figures' reproducible source is `make_figures.py`; exact objects and conventions are in `figures/geometry.json`. Native PNG and SVG files retain their full resolution.

The compact-set orientation/right-cap theorem and ordinary de Rham comparison are used under their stated hypotheses. Identification with a separately specified canonical rational cochain connecting map, integral descent, rational-form spanning, the particular affine C8 representative and component constancy require the corresponding comparison and geometric arguments.
