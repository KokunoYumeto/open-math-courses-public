# Reproduce the Euler checks and figures

Run with Python, mpmath, NumPy and Matplotlib:

    python -B -X utf8 Euler-check-and-figures.py

The script checks the falling Euler normal form and addition identity with exact rational arithmetic, the reciprocal phase shifts and full cubic coefficients, the exact separating cone, mixed reciprocal derivatives and the analytic particular solution with resonant and complex degrees. Independent numerical differentiation uses 90 decimal digits. Finite checks supplement the complete mathematical proofs and eight full solutions.

Two editable PNG/SVG diagrams show the exact radial/covector geometry and the proved positive-ray and crossing fibers. The geometry JSON specifies coordinates, the cone angle, flow parameters, arbitrary positive covector scale and exclusion of zero. SVG dates are omitted and the hash salt is fixed. The font licence is included.

Original content is CC0. Linked proofs and references retain their actual terms. General pullback, tensor, product, restriction, projection and homogeneous Fourier-wavefront interchange remain separate results.
