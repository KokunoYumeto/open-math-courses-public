The reader includes editable sources, reproducible illustrations and the prerequisite readings used by its proofs. Gaussian rescaling, both tangent directions of fiber normals, affine sections with closure after projection, convex propagation within a fiber, analytic boundary regularity at all normal frequencies, and smooth-data counterexamples are proved with worked geometric examples. The Fourier–Laplace range for every compact convex real carrier is constructed by Laplace contours, moment recovery and rotated boxes; examples explain infinite point derivatives and nonconvex-carrier limitations. Gaussian reconstruction supplies global derivative-growth representatives modulo analytic functions and the global regular-difference characterization of wavefront sets. Smooth extension at every finite order, square-variable descent with parameters, coordinate parity, exchange invariants and smooth flat majorants are constructed explicitly. Exact distributional extension orders are determined for powers with inverse-power oscillation, and a real phase giving an order-one extension is constructed for every continuously differentiable amplitude.
