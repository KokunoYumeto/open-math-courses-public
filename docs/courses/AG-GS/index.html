Group schemes

Seven lessons on group schemes and Hopf algebras, Lie algebras and smoothness, field groups, quotients and torsors, diagonalizable groups and tori, abelian varieties and Néron models. Includes complete arguments, worked examples, exercises and solutions.

  1. Group schemes, actions and Hopf algebras
  2. Lie algebras and smoothness of group schemes
  3. Group schemes over a field
  4. Quotients and torsors
  5. Diagonalizable groups and groups of multiplicative type
  6. Abelian varieties
  7. Néron models
