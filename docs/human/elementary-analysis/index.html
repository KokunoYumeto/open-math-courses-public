Open Mathematics Courses

Elementary analysis

Foundations based on work by the Open Logic Project and Jiří Lebl, with identified AI teaching contributions.

  1. Constructing the real numbers — Cauchy sequences, cuts, field operations, order, completeness and uniqueness, starting from rational arithmetic.
  2. Real analysis on closed intervals — completeness, extreme and intermediate values, differentiation and the two directions of calculus.
  3. The complex exponential and the circle — series, exact periods, polar coordinates and continuous arguments.
  4. Laurent series and homogeneous projections — annuli, product expansions, coordinate extensions and the homogeneous ordered complex.

Source and edition: Real numbers · Real analysis · Complex exponential
